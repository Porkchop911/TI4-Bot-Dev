# Review of the three extraction formats

Reviewed 2026-10-14. Read all three drafts; checked source samples and referenced segment IDs. This is not an exhaustive current-rules review.

| Draft | Size | Strength | Limitation |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Hacan | 2,505 words; 28 recommendations | Conditional advice, disagreement, components and exact cited spans | Some duplication; strategic-end metadata differs from topic map; point 1 range clips the last cited segment by 0.32 seconds |
| Letnev | 9,220 words; 127 bullet lines | Strong source detail, edition caveats and contested advice; sampled mechanics aligned | Too much duplication/anecdote for a repeatable summary; preserve it as research backup |
| Sol | 1,198 words; 23 recommendations | Meets its original bounded task, readable and focused | Compression introduced substantive errors; approximate citations make spot-checking harder |

Sol corrections: point 1's extra benefit need not be another technology; point 2 confuses taking Politics/Leadership with following a secondary, and Politics does not grant research; point 20's Orbital Drop/home build gets halfway toward Mechanize the Military, not a completed setup. Publication date exists in the catalog (2023-12-06). Its approximate timestamps were explicitly allowed by the Sol prompt, so that is not noncompliance. Hacan verification was precedent, not an implicit instruction retroactively governing other tasks.

Selected format: readable conditional summary (~1,600–2,400 words) plus one compact structured claim list with exact source ranges and component references. Both outputs derive from the same claims. Distinct openings, conflicts, counters and component mechanisms earn space; repeated takeaways and exhaustive component inventories do not. The coordinator batches mechanical checks; workers perform one semantic pass. Earlier drafts are preserved.
