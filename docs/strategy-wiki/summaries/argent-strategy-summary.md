# Argent Flight strategy summary

**SCPT 289 · Super Argent Flight Guide · 2023-03-08.** Draft synthesis of the complete verified ASR transcript. Compatibility with current rules is unreviewed; speech has no speaker attribution.

## Strategic thesis

Argent’s early strength is cheap reach: two two-move capacity destroyers make early control and a custodians attempt accessible. The guide pairs this with Sarween Tools and Plasma Scoring, then favors Strike Wing Alpha II and PDS II. The advantage can fade, so plan by round three for a stronger fleet and a bonus-point path. The hosts differ on card rankings and how aggressively to pursue alternatives after missing custodians; those disagreements remain conditional.

## Component connections

Sarween Tools and Aerie Hololattice form a production pairing (C01, C12; 224, 585–586s). Plasma Scoring and the unlocked commander can make a home PDS roll more threatening (C07–C08; 741–742, 1515s). PDS II and Strike Wing Alpha II cover distinct risks: deterrence and efficient fleet strength (C13; 733–738s). Fleet Logistics can support a hero reposition when the fleet is staged first (C18; 3255–3342s). The Jol-Nar alliance exchange can amplify Argent’s PDS network, but requires a political deal (4347–4381s).
