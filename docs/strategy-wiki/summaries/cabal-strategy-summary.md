# Vuil’Raith Cabal strategy summary

This is a strategy guide, not a current-rules ruling. It reflects SCPT 302 (2023-06-07) and the supplied complete ASR transcript; expansion and ruleset compatibility remain unreviewed. The transcript contains recognition errors, and the speakers revise views or disagree. Treat rules-sensitive readings as episode interpretations. Captured plastic is not trade goods, preparation is not scoring, and an action-phase stall is not a formal pass.

Cabal trades ordinary trade-good income for reach, production and captured units. Its visible threat can isolate it; durable board presence still offers no dependable route to the tenth point.

Construction is the usual round-one choice for the second dock. Leadership can outrank it when tokens constrain builds and movement; Trade or Diplomacy can substitute when their likely proceeds support production. Vortex is the usual early technology when nearby targets are worthwhile. These are defaults conditioned by slice, map, objectives and table response.

### C18

Do not delay the third dock while seeking a perfect site. It opens Vortex and hero targets, broadens movement and creates more build options; adjacency to useful systems matters most. **Apply when:** Place to expand reach and objective options; exact placement is map-dependent. **Limit:** The guide rejects one universal location. **Evidence:** 61:29.28–61:31.04 (s001215); 83:07.04–83:09.24 (s001622); 83:23.76–83:27.80 (s001629).

### C19

Vortex is the usual early technology because it captures a unit from reinforcements adjacent to a dock without winning combat. Delay it if nearby targets are low value and another upgrade better serves the opening. **Apply when:** Research early when a worthwhile target improves the build plan. **Limit:** Target value is board-specific; ranking is opinion. **Evidence:** 38:57.72–39:01.64 (s000791); 39:06.48–39:13.18 (s000793); 39:43.62–39:47.42 (s000801).

### C21

### C27

### C30

Cabal can prepare for stage-one control and upgrade objectives through movement, docks and captured units, but preparation is not a point until the requirement is met. Ground control needs more planning than fleets in empty space. **Apply when:** Match units and timing to the objective; do not count likely access as scoring. **Limit:** Broad advice, not a claim every stage one is easy. **Evidence:** 90:51.32–90:56.62 (s001779); 91:08.00–91:15.94 (s001782); 91:26.30–91:29.74 (s001785).

### C31

Cabal can be hard to remove once spread across the board, but the hosts say finding the 10th point is difficult and stage twos can be harder; they identify no reliable late-game closer. **Apply when:** Keep a route to bonus points or stage twos, adapting to revealed objectives; extra plastic does not force a win. **Limit:** Tournament review is selective and anecdotal; no repeatable Cabal line is claimed. **Evidence:** 91:57.64–92:02.40 (s001793); 93:24.34–93:29.32 (s001823); 94:48.58–94:49.34 (s001851); 97:56.24–97:59.06 (s001909); 99:44.72–99:49.30 (s001953).

### C32

Target access is a Cabal constraint: Vortex selects an opponent’s non-structure unit adjacent to a Dimensional Tear and captures a matching unit from their reinforcements, while the hero checks units in or adjacent to a Tear. The hosts note Vortex can be poor when opponents keep their units away. **Apply when:** Assess nearby targets and dock placement before valuing either capture effect. **Limit:** The Vortex example is one game; target availability is map-dependent. **Evidence:** 35:57.64–36:00.44 (s000729); 36:00.88–36:04.56 (s000730); 36:04.56–36:06.56 (s000731); 36:06.70–36:08.26 (s000732); 36:08.26–36:11.18 (s000733); 36:11.18–36:16.36 (s000734); 38:53.20–38:57.72 (s000790); 38:57.72–39:01.64 (s000791); 39:01.64–39:06.48 (s000792); 39:34.78–39:37.96 (s000798).

### C33

The guide does not settle whether Cabal should fight or wait: one host values force for unusual points, while another recounts success with good objectives and restraint. Read fleet projection as leverage, not an order to attack every neighbor. **Apply when:** Fight for points or useful captures when responses are manageable; otherwise economy and timing may preserve a route. **Limit:** This is an explicit disagreement shaped by different play styles. **Evidence:** 44:55.88–45:00.34 (s000900); 45:25.22–45:32.50 (s000906); 47:14.36–47:16.64 (s000951); 49:42.06–49:49.92 (s001012).
