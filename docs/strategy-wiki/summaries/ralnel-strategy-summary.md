# Ral Nel Consortium strategy summary

Ral Nel’s strength is flexibility: structures can move outside normal patterns, while action cards and tradeables can create opportunistic turns. That flexibility does not guarantee tempo, capacity or ground combat. The hosts described a faction whose unusual tools shine when an objective, map and tech path align; otherwise, its weak home production and limited punch remain real constraints.

Source: [SCPT 434: Ral Nel Consortium Beginner’s Guide](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/), published 2025-12-10. The transcript is complete and checksum-verified. Ruleset compatibility was not reviewed. ASR spellings were normalized only when a component match was clear; disputed interpretations remain marked as provisional.

## Opening and development

**C01.** Avoid a fixed faction script. Structure movement and action cards create unusual openings only when the objective, map and technology cooperate. One host’s provisional rating was around B tier, with room to revise; the other was skeptical that versatility covered the weak home system and other gaps. Treat that evaluation as opinion, not a stable ranking. ([5142.47–5189.71s; s001187–s001196](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C02.** AI Development Algorithm was the speakers’ safer starting technology in a vacuum because it opens both faction technologies and flexible upgrades. Psychoarchaeology can be better with useful skips. Neural Motivator is a purposeful card-fishing alternative, especially if seeking Overrule, but extra cards may run into hand limits, and cards you play do not feed Data Skimmer. ([2250.02–2319.38s; s000502–s000514](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/); [2330.68–2434.40s; s000519–s000537](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C03.** Plan around a lean home economy: the guide describes three total resources and limited production, alongside a three-influence planet that can help fund an early command token. Slice wealth, tech skips and what you must build can change the first-round choice. The planet names are unclear in ASR, so this summary keeps the recommendation general. ([2450.38–2511.76s; s000541–s000553](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C20.** Give Construction serious consideration when a second space dock would solve the home production cap. It can leave one dock home while another moves forward. The speakers liked it after Trade or Technology in some pick orders and noted that following Construction is also possible. Nanomachines places PDS, but it does not replace the production needed for ships. ([5330.63–5368.91s; s001217–s001223](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/); [5444.44–5509.62s; s001239–s001251](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C22.** The repeated technology direction is practical red-blue mobility: Dark Energy Tap, Gravity Drive and Carrier II, with Fighter II or Dreadnought II as possible additions. This supports exploration and delivery of ground forces. Add Nanomachines or Linkship II when the board rewards them; speakers cautioned that the green-yellow breakthrough does not line up neatly with Ral Nel’s red-blue needs. ([6000.00–6092.08s; s001369–s001390](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/); [6099.42–6144.52s; s001393–s001402](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

## Structure flexibility and combat

**C05.** Miniaturization is the faction’s defining lever. Ships can transport structures without spending capacity, then place them on planets you control in the system at the end of the tactical action. This can move a dock or PDS forward and make structure objectives easier to prepare. The guide says structures in space cannot use their unit abilities, and a carried dock cannot produce during that action. Its ruling about structures left alone in space was explicitly tentative. ([2661.60–2728.42s; s000581–s000591](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/); [2737.82–2773.56s; s000596–s000604](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C06.** Moving the home space dock toward the slice center can improve production reach, but it is a real trade. New ships may need to split between home defense and the front, and the empty home system becomes more vulnerable. One speaker regretted not moving it because the production cap hurt; another still called the relocation risky. Move it when the added reach repays the exposure and transport burden. ([2774.26–2829.28s; s000605–s000616](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C07.** Nanomachines is chiefly an off-tempo way to place PDS on planets you control. Once useful PDS are out, its action-card exchange is another option; repairing damaged units is a situational fallback the speaker had not needed. The payoff is placing structures beyond ordinary Construction timing, not that all three modes are equally valuable. ([3240.00–3311.80s; s000719–s000733](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/); [3330.98–3363.08s; s000739–s000748](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C04.** Survival Instinct can reinforce a system an opponent activates if you have eligible ships in adjacent systems without your command tokens. It may deter an attack or pull distant ships into a defense, but the hosts found few good windows in their games. Positioning and map geometry matter; treat it as a situational response, not dependable fleet capacity. ([2527.98–2589.96s; s000558–s000567](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/); [2597.40–2649.08s; s000569–s000578](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C08.** Linkship can use a transported structure’s space-cannon ability in the same system. The hosts rejected the broader reading that Linkship copies PDS II’s adjacent-system reach: under their stated interpretation, bring the PDS into the target system. They said the alternative would transform the faction, so this is a dated podcast ruling rather than verified current rules. ([2876.14–2925.96s; s000630–s000640](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/); [2939.20–2996.24s; s000645–s000663](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C09.** The same episode’s current ruling was that Experimental Battlestation does not activate Linkship: a space dock that “may use” space cannon does not gain the ability Linkship references. One host thought this interaction could be clarified differently later. Keep that uncertainty attached rather than treating the podcast’s reading as settled. ([3168.76–3234.50s; s000702–s000717](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C10.** Linkship II improves movement and lets each Linkship trigger the same transported structure, creating local space control. It remains a space-combat investment, however. When technology is scarce, the hosts often preferred red-blue mobility because Carrier II helps deliver the mechs and infantry needed to take planets. Linkship II does not solve Ral Nel’s ground-combat weakness. ([3031.60–3084.86s; s000675–s000684](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/); [3106.30–3163.78s; s000690–s000700](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C11.** Last Dispatch can retreat and destroy an opposing ship that lacks Sustain Damage. With Watchful Ojz, the speakers saw a possible earlier exit line. The target limitation matters: a ship that possesses Sustain Damage does not become eligible simply because it has already used that ability. Treat the timing discussion as the guide’s interpretation, not an independent rules check. ([3426.96–3508.42s; s000767–s000784](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C12.** Alarums can pull up to two ground forces from planets in adjacent systems after a combat round on their planet. A mech near a border can therefore surprise an opponent or help reach a control objective, if nearby planets have forces to move. The hosts said it cannot pull from another planet in the same system, and the newly moved forces cannot chain a second mech pull in that timing window. ([3548.40–3620.00s; s000798–s000811](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/); [3621.14–3669.68s; s000812–s000823](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/); [3701.40–3769.12s; s000838–s000863](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

## Cards, deals and scoring

**C13.** Nano Link Permit is a tradeable setup tool. After the buyer activates, it moves eligible adjacent structures onto planets they control in the active system; the hosts said the “after you activate” window lets the buyer build from a moved dock. That can stage a Mecatol play or improve forward production. Arrange the purchase before the planned use instead of promising immediate play. ([3781.14–3812.08s; s000867–s000873](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/); [3863.00–3909.42s; s000885–s000896](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C14.** Data Skimmer rewards opponents who spend action cards during the action phase: while Ral Nel has not passed, their discards accumulate on it, and passing lets Ral Nel keep one. Your own played cards do not go there, nor do discards from other phases. The engine can provide a late option, but only if opponents spend useful cards before you pass. ([4005.96–4106.26s; s000928–s000955](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/); [4109.72–4161.54s; s000957–s000967](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C15.** Data Skimmer’s pile is public, which changes the value of a card once you take it. Opponents can hold Sabotage or change their timing because they know you have a powerful card such as Overrule. The hosts suggested Last Bastion Alliance as a valuable defense and contrasted this with Yssaril’s hidden hand. Data Skimmer creates value while making that value easier to counter. ([5831.96–5892.24s; s001334–s001345](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/); [5892.76–5958.42s; s001346–s001359](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C16.** Kan Kip Rel’s agent draws two action cards, lets you keep one and gives the other to another player. Use that choice to create a stall and invite a deal: an opponent may value the specific card or simply appreciate receiving one. The speakers were unsure whether you may reveal cards during the ability and said hand limits are checked after it resolves; neither detail was settled. ([4182.22–4226.76s; s000976–s000984](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/); [4240.10–4327.62s; s000987–s001007](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/); [4340.02–4396.74s; s001011–s001027](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/); [4427.00–4462.82s; s001037–s001042](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C17.** Watchful Ojz requires you to be last to pass, so do not budget its retreat ability as an automatic early-game tool. Action cards may help, but the speakers still found the unlock difficult in one game and said it may not happen. It is useful when available, but it is not the essential engine of the faction. ([4554.00–4628.02s; s001064–s001082](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/); [4631.40–4692.36s; s001084–s001096](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C18.** Director Nel can reopen a post-pass action window after the last player passes, yielding two command tokens and an action card. Save it for a worthwhile move when possible: the hosts noted it might secure a Prove Endurance window, while one speaker had also used it simply for tokens and a card. Once you pass, ordinary turn actions stop until the hero unpasses you; consider enemy attack timing. ([4693.36–4799.98s; s001097–s001120](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C19.** Aim for a midgame Mecatol Rex or Imperial point rather than assuming early custodians. Structure movement can prepare a dock, and tradeables can recruit support or a useful card. But the faction’s space cannon clears space without winning ground combat; opponents can deny the point. Build tempo and ground support first, and use a deal when another player can supply the missing punch. ([5402.00–5416.10s; s001230–s001231](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/); [5655.92–5717.60s; s001283–s001297](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/); [5728.08–5769.34s; s001304–s001313](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C25.** Structure objectives are a natural scoring lane. Nanomachines can add PDS and Miniaturization can move structures to planets you control, making unusual preparations possible. These actions can make a stage-one or stage-two condition achievable, but preparing structures is not the same as scoring: every condition must be met at the scoring window. The episode’s objective name was ASR-corrupted, so the advice stays general. ([2661.60–2764.52s; s000581–s000602](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

## Late-game posture and limits

**C21.** After round one, Warfare can open structure and movement tricks, including a plan to stage or enter the Fracture. The hosts saw it as a way to compensate for Ral Nel’s weak natural access, not a guarantee. Even Linkship II movement does not supply the capacity and ground forces needed to claim distant planets. ([5559.34–5652.64s; s001263–s001280](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C24.** Biostims is a possible supplement if Nanomachines has several useful exhausted modes and a green path fits the game; it could let you use those options again. The speakers called it interesting but explicitly did not recommend it every game. Movement may matter more when the choice is between another flexible tool and delivering forces. ([6263.54–6332.82s; s001431–s001447](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C23.** Graviton becomes more appealing only under a broader Linkship/PDS II interpretation that the hosts rejected as their current ruling. In that hypothetical, removing capacity ships could help Linkships win space combat. The premise is disputed, and the speakers say ground combat is the more persistent weakness, so this is a speculative branch rather than a baseline plan. ([6842.60–6899.64s; s001555–s001568](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/); [6899.64–6978.16s; s001569–s001587](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C26.** Mecatol is easier to stage than the Fracture in the hosts’ games. Fracture success depended on drawing the right cards, reaching the area and having enough capacity and ground forces. A forward dock might help stage ships, but the speakers had not executed their hoped-for line. Treat a special Data Skimmer card as an opening when it appears, not a reliable route. ([4830.00–4913.26s; s001121–s001140](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/); [4920.62–4975.49s; s001143–s001155](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

**C27.** The hosts’ preferred posture was to stay outside the center of attention and preserve a surprise scoring window. That only works if you keep enough defense: after passing, you cannot scramble in response to attacks until Signal Intrusion is used. Public Data Skimmer cards also let the table anticipate your move. Their tier assessment remained tentative, with disagreement about how much versatility offsets the weak home system. ([6534.41–6631.64s; s001491–s001509](https://spacecatspeaceturtles.podbean.com/e/434-ral-nel-consortium-beginners-guide/)).

## Source uncertainties

The transcript sometimes renders “Ral Nel” as “Ral Nell” or “Raoul Nell”; “alarm” is the mech Alarum. Component names were normalized only when the reference matched. Linkship reach, unattended structures, retreat timing, card reveals and hand-limit timing are provisional discussions in the episode. The hosts also preserved disagreement about the faction’s competitive ceiling, and several plans were explicitly untested or card-dependent.
