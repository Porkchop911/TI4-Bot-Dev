# Manual notes: The Emirates of Hacan

## Decision guide: turn income into a scoring position

Maintained synthesis, 2026-10-15. These decision questions reorganize the existing draft claims; they add no verified rules or training labels. Source: SCPT 360 (2024-07-17). Ruleset compatibility remains unreviewed. Claim IDs below refer to the [structured evidence and exact episode spans](../summaries/hacan.json); the [readable guide](../summaries/hacan-strategy-summary.md) retains the full discussion.

| Decision | Favor this when | Change course when | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trade as the opening pick | The table will transact and the income buys units, positioning or a score | A different card resolves the actual token, production or timing shortage; estimated trade income is table-dependent | C01, C05, C08 |
| Gravity Drive, then a useful QDN route | Mobility or strategy-card access will change a scoring window | The yellow research commitment delays essential units or has no useful swap; Politics remains an alternative | C07, C09 |
| Extra income research | Its prerequisite, action and token costs are unusually cheap and income is still valuable | Cash already exceeds what production and useful actions can convert; the guide normally skips Production Biomes | C10 |
| Defensive production | Home is threatened and a screen or ground presence is missing | The actual threat demands a different unit mix; cash alone does not increase the production allowance | C02, C21, C28 |
| Space Dock II before the hero build | Bonus research arrives around rounds two or three and the added capacity will be used | The timing has passed or another upgrade has an immediate job | C13, C19 |
| A planet sale | The point or position gained exceeds the lost economy and a safe mech destination exists | Losing control or a production site would undermine the scoring route | C06, C15 |

Before committing to a large build, separate three questions: can Hacan pay, can it produce the needed units, and will those units protect or reach the relevant position? The guide's central tension is abundant cash alongside a vulnerable split home and limited production. Its hero discount does not by itself solve that tension. [C01–C02, C13, C21]

Against Hacan, inspect the home defense and the timing of an actual QDN scoring threat rather than reacting to wealth alone. Against Sardakk, the guide explicitly warns that controlling the sky may not be enough. Hacan's proposed bait-and-rebound finish remains speculative and should not become a default line. [C24, C27–C28]
