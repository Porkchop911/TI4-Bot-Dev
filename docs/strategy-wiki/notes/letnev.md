# Manual notes: The Barony of Letnev

## Decision guide: resources, movement and tokens are separate budgets

Maintained synthesis, 2026-10-15, from SCPT 463 (2026-07-01). This guide includes Thunder's Edge mechanics, Graveliche and the Fracture; do not transplant those lines into a setup without them. Current engine/rules compatibility remains unreviewed. Claim IDs refer to the [structured evidence and exact episode spans](../summaries/letnev.json); see the [readable guide](../summaries/letnev-strategy-summary.md).

| Decision | Favor this when | Change course when | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Secure Graveliche before ordinary extra research | The breakthrough is in the setup and shared movement improves the opening | A concrete scoring need, access problem or secret cost changes the comparison | C02–C03, C08 |
| Fast escort with slower ships | The ships begin together and the shared movement reaches a useful destination | The fleet is split or ground forces and tokens cannot support the trip | C07, C24, C26 |
| Influence and Leadership access | Repeated activations, secondaries and token replacement constrain the plan | More resources would solve the actual immediate shortage; home resources alone do not supply influence | C04, C20 |
| Shift fleet tokens toward tactics | Armada still leaves the intended fleet legal and the activation has a job | Chasing a zero fleet pool merely creates action shortages or stalls | C15–C16 |
| Pay for combat tools | Rerolls, a deployed mech or sustain support changes an important fight | The spending removes the units, tokens or infantry needed for the follow-up | C14, C17, C20, C24 |
| Build Arc Secundus | Its shield removal and repair matter in the coming fight | The actual opponent negates sustain value or the investment has no timely use | C18, C27 |

Before committing to the guide's center/Fracture schedule, check four things separately: shared movement from the starting system, command tokens, landing forces and reinforcement access. The proposed round-two Mecatol/round-three Styx pace assumes all of them. It is a guide-era opportunity, not a timetable for every setup. [C07, C24, C26]

Six home resources can conceal a recurring cash shortage: rerolls, mech deployment, ships and tokens compete for funding. Commander sustain income is conditional on the unlock and actual sustain use; the guide warns that spectacular repair loops require outside components and resources. They should not anchor the ordinary economy. [C01, C20, C22–C23]

Against Barony, inspect its infantry and activation budget as well as fleet strength. The guide names Crimson's flagship as a severe sustain matchup concern and offers no reliable answer in that contested position. Preserve that uncertainty rather than turning the counter into a solved matchup. [C16, C24, C27]
