# Manual notes: The Federation of Sol

## Decision guide: buy useful expansion before optional research

Maintained synthesis, 2026-10-15, from SCPT 328 (2023-12-06), in its Prophecy of Kings six-player context. Current-rules compatibility remains unreviewed. Claim IDs refer to the [structured evidence and exact episode spans](../summaries/sol.json); see the [readable guide](../summaries/sol-strategy-summary.md) for the complete draft.

| Decision | Favor this when | Change course when | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gravity Drive before additional early technology | It enables the ordinary expansion plan and extra research would displace useful units | A revealed objective or slice creates an immediate different research need | C17 |
| Advanced Carrier II | Movement and capacity are limiting delivery of infantry and fighters | Another upgrade has a specific objective or matchup job; do not confuse starting capacity with upgraded movement | C02–C03, C29 |
| Politics secondary | Action cards matter, hand space remains, and the token has no more urgent use | The hand is full or the token is needed elsewhere; following Politics is distinct from taking its primary | C05, C19 |
| Orbital Drop | Two infantry or the additional action changes defense or timing | It merely consumes a strategy token without improving the position | C04 |
| Leadership primary | The additional tokens fund useful actions and follows | The planned uses do not justify the pick; surplus tokens are not automatically productive | C05, C18 |
| Warfare opening | Money, useful secondaries and a second movement opportunity are all available | Any link depends on unavailable timing or an uncooperative deal | C23–C24 |

For an expansion decision, write down the destination, ships, infantry and payment before buying optional research. The guide favors transported ground-force volume over a routine Spec Ops II detour. A larger carrier only helps if its cargo and movement can be used. This is a planning checklist derived from those priorities, not a claim that every expansion beats technology. [C01–C03, C06, C17]

Separate objective preparation from scoring. Orbital Drop plus a home build is described as only halfway toward Mechanize the Military. Likewise, the round-one Custodians double-tech line is contested theorycraft with demanding prerequisites, not a standard opening. [C24, C31]

Plan the hero around what can act after board tokens are removed; the tokens return to reinforcements. Ground defense becomes stronger with the unlocked commander and agent, while bombardment remains a named counter. Those advantages depend on the relevant timing and unlock, not merely on owning Sol infantry. [C09–C12]
