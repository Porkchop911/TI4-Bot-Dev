# Manual notes: The Universities of Jol-Nar

## Decision guide: fix the opening before monetizing research

Maintained synthesis, 2026-10-15, from SCPT 340 (2024-02-29). Current-rules compatibility remains unreviewed. Claim IDs refer to the [structured evidence and exact episode spans](../summaries/jolnar.json); see the [readable guide](../summaries/jolnar-strategy-summary.md).

| Decision | Favor this when | Change course when | Evidence |

An infantry fix is complete only when the units can participate in the intended expansion. Track the build window, available transport and destination together. This checklist synthesizes the guide's opening timing warning and later emphasis on delivery upgrades; it does not establish a legal continuation for a particular state. [C02, C08, C17]

The hero makes some non-upgrade technology choices reversible; it does not make unit upgrades interchangeable. The guide's round-five technology portfolio is a preparation target, and the relevant objectives still need to appear and be scoreable. Do not replace Anti-Mass automatically if a late objective or route still needs it. [C14, C18–C19]

E-Res can make casual activations expensive, but it does not stop a committed endgame attack. Pair deterrence with actual ground defense: the guide recommends infantry numbers and distributed mech protection against Fragile's downside. Deals that improve an opponent's fleet must be valued against this defensive position. [C05–C07, C21]
