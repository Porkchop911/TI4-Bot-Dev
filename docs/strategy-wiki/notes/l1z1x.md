# Manual notes: The L1Z1X Mindnet

## Decision guide: make the invasion earn a point

Maintained synthesis, 2026-10-15, from SCPT 347 (2024-04-17). Current-rules compatibility remains unreviewed. Claim IDs refer to the [structured evidence and exact episode spans](../summaries/l1z1x.json); see the [readable guide](../summaries/l1z1x-strategy-summary.md).

| Decision | Favor this when | Change course when | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Influence and a blue skip in the draft | Repeated activations and the research path need support | Neither is available and the faction's combat tools have no plausible scoring role | C01–C02 |
| Diplomacy opening | Refreshing the five-resource home planet funds a useful second spend | Order or timing prevents using the refresh; Leadership or cooperative Trade addresses the real shortage better | C03–C05 |
| Warfare opening | Extra reach serves the map or a revealed objective | More spread merely weakens the fleet concentration available later | C06 |
| Core movement and transport upgrades | Gravity Drive, Super Dreadnought II or Carrier II gives a concrete fleet job | Research costs consume the units or activations needed to exploit it | C08–C10 |
| Capture an enemy structure | The planet is reachable, conquerable and useful for production or a structure objective | Taking it exposes the fleet, leaves no scoring route, or fails the objective's actual location requirements | C12, C19, C21–C22, C25 |
| Save the hero for a late reposition | A useful destination lacks other players' ships and the follow-up matters | Opponents occupy the required staging systems | C17 |

For an Assimilate plan, inspect the whole sequence: reach the system, bring enough ground forces, take the planet, then identify the production or scoring opportunity. The captured structure's value depends on that continuation. An attractive dock on the map is not evidence that the invasion succeeds. This is a synthesis of the guide's capture mechanism and its warnings against unproductive war. [C12–C14, C19, C21–C22]

Harrow and the flagship strengthen particular fights; neither replaces infantry, command tokens or objective prerequisites. Keep the fleet mobile enough to score after a successful attack. A winslay that strands L1 outside the scoring pack can serve somebody else's finish. [C13, C15, C19–C22]

Opponents can restrict hero destinations by occupying relevant systems. Bargaining also changes the decision: the guide favors equitable point-enabling trades over low-return extortion that damages future relations. [C17–C18]
