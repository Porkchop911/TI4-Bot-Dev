# Manual notes: The Xxcha Kingdom

## Decision guide: unlock spending power without losing the opening

Maintained synthesis, 2026-10-15, from SCPT 264 (2022-09-14), using the hero interpretation discussed there. Guide age and hero-version compatibility matter; current rules and engine behavior remain unreviewed. Claim IDs refer to the [structured evidence and exact episode spans](../summaries/xxcha.json); see the [readable guide](../summaries/xxcha-strategy-summary.md).

| Decision | Favor this when | Change course when | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early objective route toward the hero | The public and secret objectives have credible near-term scoring paths | A theoretical fast unlock would require abandoning necessary ships or an unattainable objective | C01–C02, C05 |
| Diplomacy | A useful refresh can be spent and Peace Accords has an eligible adjacent unoccupied planet | Trade has a better concrete payout or no useful refresh/eligible acquisition exists | C07–C10, C16 |
| Round-two Imperial | Two stage-one objectives can be prepared and card access permits an extra public score | Only a secret draw is available; drawing a card does not score it | C13 |
| Additional early research | An objective, slice or timing problem justifies it | Double-teching displaces the opening ships needed to survive and expand | C14–C15 |
| Red route toward War Suns | A longer game leaves time to buy movement, bombardment and meaningful fleet power | An immediate mobility need, skip or defensive objective favors another route | C22–C27 |
| Save strategy tokens for late tools | Quash or faction technologies have a concrete use | Repeated speculative uses consume the budget before an important window | C17, C19 |

Keep three ledgers separate after the hero unlock: spending value when exhausting planets, printed values used by unrelated requirements, and production capacity. The guide's hero increases qualifying spending power. It does not raise printed production or automatically unlock the commander. More money can therefore coexist with a severe cash-to-units bottleneck. [C03–C04, C22, C27]

For the proposed Imperial opening, name each objective and its scoring window. The Custodians point does not satisfy the hero's objective prerequisite as described in the guide, and an additional secret draw still needs a feasible score. This prevents a plausible point total from being mistaken for a completed unlock. [C02, C05–C06, C13]

Refresh effects need both valuable planets and time to spend them. A longer recovery plan also needs enough force to stop another player finishing first. Opponents can withhold early help or coordinate votes, but the guide treats full-table cooperation against Xxcha as uncertain. [C09, C18, C29–C32]
