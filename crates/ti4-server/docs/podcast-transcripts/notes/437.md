# SCPT 437 2025 Faction Tier List (2025-12-31; Hunter, Blasto, JJ + panel) — own transcription base.en
- 0:05 Arborec: improved, C tier (panel agrees). Breakthrough (token lift; costs a secret discard to unlock?) transformational. High ceiling (mobile production, hero), low floor: nothing sellable, mediocre home, planet-dependent economy.
- 0:08 Argent Flight: high F/low D. Consistent (movement, decent home spend, good at stage I) but no finisher: "nine-point faction". Hero worse (can't hero then lift token after Warfare changes). Breakthrough synergy decent: yellow start -> Gravity Drive, Fleet Logistics, Light/Wave. JJ: can fix bad slice; custodians via Gravity Drive.
- 0:12 Barony of Letnev: C to low-A (disagreement). Gravleash Maneuvers breakthrough gives plastic a purpose: take Rex/Styx earlier and stronger than anyone. Weak without the Fracture; strong token economy early (start +2 tokens); bad at spend objectives, good at controls. Hunter: good at round-2 Imperial play; can sit on Rex for influence; can take a neighbour's home R1 if equidistant systems not blocked — "block your equidistants" vs Barony.
- 0:16 Barony settled at B (R2 custodians/Imperial into Fracture is a winning line).
- 0:18 Clan of Saar: S/A (Blasto A: meta heat, take Diplomacy to protect planets when dogpiled). Indirectly buffed by TE (Construction changes, Fracture, R4/R5-action wins). Best at racing R1-R2. Can be eliminated (home planets takeable).
- 0:21 Council Keleres: B. Hunter hates Executive Order (extra agenda phases = roulette). Tech start good; Agency Supply Network one of best techs; breakthrough gives X-89 (yellow-green) and frees first tech for Gravity Drive. Pattern: root on Rex with lots of infantry + X-89 -> nobody touches Rex or home.
- 0:25 Crimson Rebellion: S, #1-2 in game; won every tournament game seen. Untakeable home, start with breakthrough, DT start, bonus movement, great flagship; commander alliance gives huge money (ally also benefits — be the Crimson ally). Example: "spend 6 tokens" stage II scored via Imperial first action by emptying fleet pool. Only Public Disgrace stops it.
- 0:31 Deepwrought Scholarate: bottom S (JJ A). Home 4/4 & coexist; nearly always X-89 via breakthrough; huge production; rich; great at stage I control objectives; weak finisher; relies on boat-floating (selling tech) — table can starve it.
- 0:35 Embers of Muaat: D (JJ high F). Starts slow (1 move); needs Gravity Drive/War Sun upgrade early; sellable promissory; drag planet; stall via Star Forge.
- 0:38 Emirates of Hacan: low A / high B (JJ hot take high). Breakthrough pays itself; Quantum Datahub Node (QDN) easier -> Imperial-type plays; war suns via synergy + hero; build 2 docks at home early (new Construction R1) + fighter screen to defend 3-planet home. Hunter worries about slayability.
- 0:45 Empyrean: high B. Not good at Styx (no combat edge, flagship can't go). Aetherstream main movement advantage; tethers can trap fleets/block Styx. PSA: don't give Empyrean ingress tokens. Frontier deck/relic fragments value.
- 0:49 Federation of Sol: S (#4/#5, below Crimson). Breakthrough (Sling Relay "party bus") — carrier full of fighters/infantry is best use of a fleet token; 4-resource home; hero reaches Styx; set up for custodians or mid-game Imperial. "Platonically set up for the TE game."
- 0:51 Firmament/Obsidian: F, bottom. "Early econ is better than late econ": nothing early (bad home, units, tech), monster only from round 3 — too late. Only with Crimson alliance siphoning: C.
- 0:57 Ghosts of Creuss: B to low A. Breakthrough transforms (Malice as new home; Sling Relay + dock on Malice -> many dreadnoughts; Wormhole Generator -> early Styx); weak start overcome quickly; taking custodians more often; easy Dread II.
- 1:00 Ghosts settled low A (under Hacan).
- 1:01 L1Z1X: high C (disagreement: Hunter ~#17 top-half-ish, Blasto C/D). Breakthrough worth getting (5-resource unlock easy); extra infantry from planets/Rex; very good at Styx with little (X-89 easy, hero teleports so only Gravity Drive needed for blue). Counter: block L1 hero/Styx approach with ships adjacent if they lack Light/Wave or Fleet Logistics. Example: L1 nearly won with 1-planet slice.
- 1:05 Last Bastion: high D (above Muaat). Very bad start, ok after dock upgrade; Galvanize faction tech + hero can hold Rex vs 5 attackers; commander (action cards can't be cancelled) strong sellable — sell it EARLY to a "hopeful gambler"; never sell to someone who asks (they hold overrule). Integrated Economy + Liberate endgame strong.
- 1:10 Mahact: S #1 (by priors). Breakthrough ~3 relics by purging techs (purge Bio-Stims, DT, Sling late); great at Styx (Star Lancers, hero vs air holder); integrated-economy faction (infantry everywhere), little blue tech needed. Plan: Imperial point, then Star Lancers to Styx.
- 1:16 Mentak: F. Cruiser III (breakthrough) still requires researching Cruiser II first; no closers; best Scar (entropic scar) beneficiary; ambush good for opportunistic grabs only. "Would rather play blue-yellow".
- 1:18 Naalu: high B (Hunter high A). Zero token good for custodians / first-turn Imperial but R5 status matters less now; breakthrough+hero gives tokens; 4 free mechs; Gift of Prescience (bribe/tie in diplomacy). Not set up for Styx.
- 1:22 Naaz-Rokha: mid A (Blasto B, JJ low S). Voltron breakthrough a trap; breakthrough's color synergy (Gravity Drive -> back to Prefab -> X-89) valuable; hero 2 relics = secret tempo catch-up; mechs excellent; strong at fighting in the Fracture round ~3 (cruiser path).
- 1:27 Nekro: high B. Faster game hurts slow starters; gets tech from Deepwrought/Jol-Nar games (but Deepwrought gives everyone tech); closed-loop economy (agent, action cards); works in high-resource slices.
- 1:32 Nomad: bottom B / high C. Decent econ; breakthrough good only with specific agents (Hacan/Xxcha/Naalu); sell Cavalry (flagship promissory) especially for Fracture fights; use hero earlier (R3-R4).
- 1:38 Ral Nel: high C, "most average faction" (#15); jack of all trades; only real closer Overrule (draws many action cards); not set up for custodians + Styx.
- 1:41 Sardakk: bottom D. Breakthrough great but needs fighting to get tokens (forever wars); good fighter with no economy -> pounced on when leading.
- 1:45 Titans of Ul: top A (JJ bottom S). Versatile; early control/structure objectives = easy street; Scar helps (AI Dev -> Saturn Engine); Construction even better now; great at Styx. Plastic start weak.
- 1:47 Jol-Nar: top A (above Titans). Gained X-89 & Magen; good at nearly every stage I objective; Strategize action cards; R2 Mageon + E-Res trick: invite others to activate home for money. Deepwrought > Jol-Nar (JJ).
- 1:51 Vuil'raith Cabal: mid A (below Titans). Go to Fracture R2 for combat + relics but don't overextend — secure own slice first, block Leaf/Thong with a cruiser; Metali Void Armaments + Duranium -> unbeatable; loud and draws heat; don't assume Cabal gets Styx.
- 1:58 Winnu: moved to top of D (impossible to rate: best component (hero) — rated A if table treats them normally, worst if table punches them). Example: Fleet Logistics + Thunder's Edge extra turns + hero = 3-4 point turn from "behind". Lesson: don't let Winnu with movement/hero near Rex go unchecked.
- 2:02 Xxcha: F (Blasto #30). Defensive abilities worthless without a way to get ahead; only good at agenda phase.
- 2:06 Yin: top C (Hunter mid B, Blasto high C). Hero to Styx reliably first; Yin Spinner + Integrated Economy pop-off; usually forced to go blue tech; good at squatting on Mecatol; commanders from breakthrough decide game.
- 2:12 Yssaril: top A (moved down from S). Action-card deck buffed (also via Mageon theft) but heavy anti-Yssaril heat; should NOT go for custodians or the Fracture — sit in slice, win last round; R5 stalling matters less now; must get Gravity Drive.
- FINAL 2025 LIST: S: Mahact, Crimson, Sol, Saar, Deepwrought, Jol-Nar | A: Yssaril, Titans, Cabal, Hacan, Naaz-Rokha, Ghosts | B: Naalu, Nekro, Empyrean, Keleres, Barony, Nomad | C: Yin, Ral Nel, L1Z1X, Arborec, Last Bastion | D: Winnu, Muaat, Sardakk | F: Argent, Mentak, Xxcha, Firmament/Obsidian.
- Later revision (ep 462, Jun 2026): Empyrean and Deepwrought underrated, Crimson slightly overrated.
