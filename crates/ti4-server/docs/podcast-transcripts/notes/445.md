# SCPT 445 Deepwrought Scholarate Beginner's Guide (2026-02-25, Hunter + JJ) — own transcription base.en, 1:20-1:54
- Rule note: coexisting doesn't give Styx control or Imperial's Rex point (only counts for objectives that say so, e.g. Occupy the Seat).
- R1 custodians line: 4/4 home planet; with Leadership follow Tech, Warfare, Diplomacy; Diplomacy refresh home -> 4 influence + one planet = 6 for custodians (especially with double-blue start + Carrier II).
- R1 cards: POLITICS high (prefer custodians top of R2 rather than over-investing R1; also unlock breakthrough via discarding action cards; fallback Imperial R2 with strong plastic). DIPLOMACY high when late pick (breakthrough unlock; leverage over tech timing), better with blue start. TRADE always great (encourages adjacency = breakthrough sharing; locks breakthrough). TECHNOLOGY medium but "Tech card ≈ Trade card" for Deepwrought (they make money and tech from both); if holding Technology: do own breakthrough first (prereqs + commander unlock), sell agent, give alliance early so trades happen before tech is played (earn full commodities). LEADERSHIP low-medium (only 2 capital ships; helps R1 custodians). CONSTRUCTION medium-low (production not a problem; redundant with Sling Relay). WARFARE low (taking only 2 systems R1 + coexisting is fine).
- "Early money is worth more than late money": first TG matters, 20 TG at game end are worthless.
- Alliance (commander) swap: give it to anyone (ideally whoever provides Gravity Drive); no need to charge.
- Tech: blue-green faction; Dock II + double green for fighters; unit upgrades Fighter II, Carrier II, Space Dock II; X-89; Fleet Logistics to win; Self-Assembly Routines; Transit Diodes (can transit FROM coexisted planet, not TO). Infantry II not needed (X-89; mechs build production).
- Weakness (JJ): no catch-up closer if Saar races ahead; okay winslayer but needs Warfare. Strength: excellent at holding a lead; "dreaded Deepwrought winslay" hard for tables. "Evil Deepwrought" (charging for everything) still works.
