# SCPT PoK-era Super Guides (2023-2024, Matt + Hunter) — own transcription base.en, guessed range 55-92% of each episode (mostly tech path / trading / late game; R1 card sections often missed)

## 360 Super Emirates of Hacan Guide (2024-07-17), 0:54-1:33
- Tech: classic blue+yellow: Gravity Drive R1, Carrier II R2, then Quantum Datahub Node (QDN) — sometimes rush QDN R3 so the table "gets used to it"; Space Dock II before the hero build if bonus tech (focused research etc.) appears early; Predictive vs Transit (hosts split); Fleet Logistics/Light/Wave for final-round plays with QDN->Imperial; AI Dev path (Carrier II + Dread II without Gravity Drive) possible but less cohesive.
- "Ethical Hacan" (Matt): facilitate everyone's deals cheaply ("a buck for everything"), be easy to work with (people then help with planet trades for control objectives); stingy Hacan = slow, unfun, loses. Buy War Machine and tech action cards.
- Late game: Hacan is slayable (3-planet home hard to defend; only infantry and fighters are good HP for defence); QDN makes Hacan "face up" — ideas: score all stage I early, Imperial+Rex so the win needs no publics; or deliberately take a hit in R4 and rebound; build plastic all game; win via deals/gambits and late surprise action cards (Signal Jamming, Experimental Battlestation).
- Mantis "win-slay carousel": each player stops the player who would win right before them in initiative/scoring order; Imperial player first (action phase), Leadership stops Imperial, etc.

## 358 Super Yssaril Tribes Guide (2024-07-03), 0:58-1:38
- R1: TRADE high (start with trade ship; tech before 3-resource home), TECHNOLOGY high (double tech with Bio-Stims, e.g. Bio-Stims + DT), WARFARE low (don't need secondary — make factions that need it take it), Construction low (wants tech).
- Core tech: Bio-Stims, Mageon Implants, Gravity Drive (Mageon by R3 > Light/Wave by R3); then DT/Antimass, Carrier II, Fleet Logistics/Light/Wave; Transparasteel Plating vs Hyper Metabolism; few unit upgrades (action cards decide combats); flagship = two dreads in one ship.
- Agent copies others' agents: "Goblin fish" with Hacan (both agents on each other each round: ~9 TG split), Naalu agent for R1 custodians, Mahact agent swaps, Jol-Nar agent.
- Late: stall everyone out, then do control objectives "like a slow magic trick"; Imperial R2 with deploy-mech on Rex is fine for Yssaril; get a support partner early; Yssaril riders strong in agenda phase. Hosts: Yssaril boring/bad for casual play.

## 340 Super Universities of Jol-Nar Strategy Guide (2024-02-29), 1:14-2:04
- E-Res Siphons: get R1 only if a neighbour will attack you early (Nekro, Mentak, Sol, Hacan); otherwise R2 (R1 tech should be sold via research agreement — "get paid for all your tech R1").
- Unit upgrades: Dread II first (-1 combat hurts cheap units most: fighters ~half as effective; dreads carry hits + bombardment), Carrier II (more ground forces needed), Space Dock II (production; or Sling Relay instead), Destroyer II situationally (AFB with commander reroll), Fighter II low. "Cowboy math": don't use battle calculator; know fleet averages; Jol-Nar must bring more stuff than others.
- Hero (tech swap) usually R5 (sometimes R4): wait until the stage II is known; drop Antimass (unless empties), Neural Motivator, Self-Assembly, maybe Gravity Drive -> Light/Wave, Fleet Logistics, Sling Relay + Bio-Stims (stalls), X-89 (with war suns), Assault Cannon (3 ships per critical system: defence and sniping), Duranium; Sarween -> Predictive/Graviton.
- Research agreement worth ~6-8/round: sell for ~3 per use so own tech is free; spread tech to weaker factions (Sardakk, Barony) rather than making a rich faction (Hacan/Sol) richer — "table boat float", not ride-or-die (rest of table stops caring if you crown someone). Use research agreement as R1 lobbying (timing with Warfare holder).
- Alliance/relationship notes (from JadiimJedi): Nomad (buy Cavalry; commander swap), Nekro (activate each other for E-Res money), Argent (swap + buy their promissory), Hacan (money for tech; Hacan shouldn't sell Trade Convoys to Jol-Nar), Mahact (buy Scepter; ground forces on home planets vs Mahact hero), Naalu (research agreement for Zayu), Sol (research agreement for Military Support), "always punch the Winnu".
- Objectives: Jol-Nar dominates tech and structure objectives (start with 3 structures; PDS II defence), hoard TG for econ objectives; control objectives harder. Win plan: stay in pack, lock speaker order for the stage II or late Imperial point; need the most plastic in the late game.

## 336 Super Clan of Saar Guide (2024-01-31, Matt + Hunter; guest advice from Imsen), 1:00-1:40
- R1 tech debate: Chaos Mapping (build at start of every action — linchpin, the earlier the more plastic) vs Gravity Drive (only if it's the difference for R1 custodians). Always get Chaos Mapping; then Carrier II (2nd blue). "You could stop there" — spend money on plastic over more tech. Bonus tech: Fleet Logistics (R5 first action: Chaos Map flagship, Diplomacy to refund, Fleet Log move out and take another system), Light/Wave (alpha wormhole asteroid home), DT (more empty systems, retreats; avoid retreat-trap between two floating factories), Dread II, Floating Factory II late.
- Imsen's tip: build INFANTRY first (R1-R2 nobody has ground forces; infantry don't clog carrier capacity; 4 infantry per planet "slug trail" also gives retreat options), pivot to fighters mid game with Carrier II. Matt saw 36 infantry across a quarter of the board.
- Play in your own slice + equidistants (no gallivanting = less heat); can play a 3-fleet-supply game with carriers full of fighters.
- Vs Saar: if you take Saar's home, leave one planet (Shard/Styx-type contingency) — taking both helps them. Saar starts with heat — accept it; buy goodwill (TG, small planet trades, promissory). Extortion: only when calling your bluff wouldn't hurt you; cheap "go be someone else's problem" payments and support swaps are easy. Let other threats (Naalu, Winnu, Mahact) stay at the top; be the hired muscle. Don't win from ahead; play tight and conservative; keep others with good tempo around.
- Objectives: weak at PDS/structure-outside-home objectives (floating factories aren't on planets) — plan an extra bonus point (Rex/Imperial) instead; Chaos Mapping hides flagship builds for secrets.

## 328 Super Federation of Sol Guide (2023-12-06), 1:06-1:44
- R1 WARFARE is viable for Sol (rare for blue-start factions): get Gravity Drive (follow tech), sell Military Support (1 TG each, as often as possible), play Warfare LAST to move again and take custodians R1 with 6 influence (Trade 3 TG + Military Support sales; Diplomacy makes it easy). "Someone has to get custodians" — others selling Sol help is rational.
- Technology for Sol: not important; double-teching R1 is "win-more"; factions with shallow tech needs should build plastic R1. Blue-start factions can use the Technology card as leverage (get free wash/refresh in return for timing). Christmas-land: double tech GD + Carrier II + Diplomacy for R1 custodians.
- Core tech: Gravity Drive + Advanced Carrier II — "you could win the whole game with just that". Supplements: Sling Relay + Bio-Stims (more start-of-turn windows to sell Military Support; sling capital ships, build only fighters/infantry at home), Fleet Logistics, Light/Wave (hero), DT, Scanlink (spend surplus on roulette, not more plastic); Spec Ops II pointless; no red tech; Fighter II/Dread II niche. Keep only as many strategy tokens as needed (Orbital Drop burns extras).
- Alliances: Ghosts (fighters via wormholes), Sardakk ("ruins the game in Sol's hands"), Cabal, Saar (swap mediocre commanders), Nomad (cheap flagship), Muaat.
- Agendas: Conventions of War good for Sol; Economic Equality bad (rich); Executive Sanctions bad; Prophecy of Ixth great; Regulated Conscription bad early.
- Objectives: good at everything except structures; nine ground forces on a planet trivial; mechanize via Orbital Drop.

## 326 Super Ghosts of Creuss Guide (2023-11-22), 1:20-1:30 read
- R1 TRADE ideal (take Malice first action + Trade -> ~15 TG for plastic, tech, Sling Relay); WARFARE acceptable/necessary at an uncooperative table (only one carrier; ghosts' builds still reach good systems); TECHNOLOGY comfy (Sling Relay -> dread to Malice); Construction and sometimes Diplomacy disliked. "Make the factions that need Warfare take it."
- Tech needs: Sling Relay R1 (or DT if you survive R1 without Sling) -> Carrier II R2; DT also guards the Creuss-gate chokepoint (one exit from home — park there so neighbours can't gum it). PDS-Creuss no longer a thing.

## 323 Super Nekro Virus Guide (2023-11-01), 1:12-1:24 read
- Closed economy (agent money, commander action cards) — Nekro can win even in a no-trade meta. Tech is expensive early (token + ship to copy), cheapest late.
- R1 (JadiimJedi pre-errata): often take WARFARE yourself (need the secondary/plastic, set up a control objective); follow Politics (keep the good action card); don't research extra tokens R1 — buy plastic first. Hosts: Trade ideal but Warfare is more important for Nekro — vs Nekro, force them to take Warfare. Leadership middling; Diplomacy only if it scores; Politics cautious R1 but speaker control later (Politics R2-R5) fits Nekro (no agenda votes); Construction low (but Nekro has a spare strategy token since it never follows Technology). Let everyone research tech (more to copy) — take Technology if nobody else will.
- Copy targets: all blue tech (Gravity Drive first), Bio-Stims combos.

## 302 Super Vuil'raith Cabal Guide (2023-06-07), 1:16-1:24 read
- Trading style: many small "cookies" (1 TG per favour, timing of a strategy card for a buck) instead of one big extortion — accumulates the same money without table heat; don't ask for a support with your plastic advantage. Neighbours often pay Cabal early so they can have a normal slice.
- Mid-game: get ALL THREE space docks out quickly (third dock somewhere adjacent to many systems — equidistant/next to Rex — for Vortex and hero); take Construction R1 and R2 (no custodians plan, good economy). Cabal has surplus resources: spend planets for influence/tokens (command tokens go further), usually two builds per round.

## 287 Super Naaz-Rokha Alliance Guide (2023-02-22), 1:32-1:42 read
- R1 custodians lines: Psychoarchaeology start -> Cruiser II (tech primary) or Gravity Drive with a blue skip; need ~6 resources + 6 influence (Trade 3 TG, hazardous explores give TG/tokens/refresh, Diplomacy). Take Diplomacy yourself only if YOU will be the one to get custodians. If another player has Diplomacy and you are early, take TECHNOLOGY (control tech timing) — "comfortable".
- R1 cards: Leadership not essential (explores give tokens); Construction for a forward dock (Distant Suns mech on the dock planet; prefer forward dock over double dock at home); Trade great; Politics fine (custodians R2 via Gravity Drive); Warfare low (would let the table suffer by leaving it untaken if it hurts several others).
- NRA too hard to punish early even when taking custodians.

## 270 Super Naalu Collective Guide (2022-10-26, Codex 3 era), 0:58-1:14 read
- Path to 10: 4 stage I + 3 secrets + support + stage II or Imperial+public; Naalu doesn't care much where speaker is (initiative 0). Imperial around R4 (after you know your secrets) — Rex point costs a secret draw; DT helps secret tempo.
- R1: DIPLOMACY favourite (R1 and again R2-R4; ready a big planet second action — e.g. a 5-value planet = 5 bucks); TRADE if first pick (primary money >> secondary; hope someone else takes Diplomacy and follow it); CONSTRUCTION for the forward dock (home production poor; agent can't be used at home) — Matt prefers it to Warfare when Warfare is otherwise untaken; LEADERSHIP low; POLITICS useless (Neural Motivator start, zero token means no need for speaker); TECHNOLOGY low (blue tech DT/Antimass later). Cards Naalu wants someone to take: Trade, Diplomacy, Warfare.
- Naalu likes boosting everybody (boat float) because it wins ties of the pack on the zero.
