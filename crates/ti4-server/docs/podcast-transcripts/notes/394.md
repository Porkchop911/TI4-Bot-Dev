# SCPT 394 Super Guide to the Agenda Phase (2025-03-12, Matt + Hunter; PoK era) — base.en 0:00-1:10 (of 136 min; timing-window walk-through, agendas themselves covered in 398, not transcribed)
- Agenda phase only after custodians taken (mostly thematic gate; R1 custodians ~30% of games). Only ~6-8 agendas per game — mostly not huge; tension comes from riders/vetoes.
- Politics holder agenda choice: if AHEAD keep "nothing burger" agendas (status quo); if BEHIND bury the nothing burgers and keep disruptive agendas ("need a stir"). Players often choose by vibe — mistake.
- Always keep at least ONE vote available going into every agenda (mainly for Mutiny); Maw of Worlds exhausting all planets can cost you; Ancient Burial Sites play ASAP (rarely matters).
- Timing: "when revealed" window = veto/quash/political secret/Diplomatic Pressure; "after revealed" = riders, Assassinate Representative (hold it until after riders are played), Sanction, Keleres Rider, Nekro Galactic Threat, Naalu agent peek, Hack Election (usually irrelevant), Insider Information (play after FIRST agenda, not second); speaker-order passes until all pass.
- Political Secret/Ceasefire: too devastating to give out at high level.
- Riders value: Imperial Rider (VP) and Politics Rider (3 action cards + speaker) biggest; Leadership Rider (3 tokens) big and underrated; Technology Rider (extra tech tempo) big; Trade (5 TG) middle; Warfare (dreadnought)/Construction/Diplomacy (Diplomacy rider can target Rex) small. Politics Rider recruits votes from players whose pick order improves; Imperial Rider is for one player only -> table votes against. Most common real agenda = "Politics Rider vs Imperial Rider". Riders change the agenda's meaning: players vote against a side with a juicy rider; playing Imperial Rider can be a poison pill to push table the other way. Sanction = poison pill (voters lose fleet token).
- Nekro Galactic Threat: feed Nekro tech passively via votes (better than being attacked); neighbours should buy in.
- Keleres Rider: announce at the top of the window so others can buy; split TG.
- Hosts dislike abstaining; Committee Formation picks elected player before votes (pair with Imperial Rider).
