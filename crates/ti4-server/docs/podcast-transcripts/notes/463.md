# SCPT 463 Ultimate Guide to the Barony of Letnev (2026-07-01, Hunter + JJ) — own transcription base.en, 1:40-2:11
- Barony (like Arborec) "1000% needs the breakthrough" (Gravleash Maneuvers). Unlock options: discard secret (least preferred), spend home system (6 resources) — then you sit out tech/warfare secondaries.
- R1 card ranking: TRADE (JJ top: money + breakthrough + still R1 custodians possible since Diplomacy is almost always picked) and POLITICS (Hunter: super high — blue start + Gravleash + Antimass: likely R2 first-action custodians / "round-2 Imperial faction"; rivals for R2 Rex: Saar, Titans (sustain vs Barony's upgraded dice) — make a deal via commander). Then Leadership/Diplomacy/Technology depending on slice; Diplomacy preferred over Leadership (control timing; refresh tech-skip planet for breakthrough). CONSTRUCTION decent (double dock at home -> 10 production; fighters + Carrier II), Hunter rates Warfare (with Gravity Drive aggressive expansion; send a destroyer with carrier) above Construction, JJ the reverse. TECHNOLOGY low: never double-tech anymore (unless it scores R1, e.g. Carrier II+Destroyer II for unit upgrades); single tech = Gravity Drive; being after Warfare lets you unlock commander first. Imperial/Warfare usually not wanted.
- PSA: people sleep on R1 custodians; Diplomacy is so commonly picked R1 that more factions should make custodians plays.
- Discarding secret for breakthrough + Imperial R2 & R3 left Hunter with 0 secrets — real cost.
- Core tech: Gravity Drive, Non-Euclidean Shielding, Carrier II (Hunter) / JJ: Destroyer II, Gravity Drive, NES, Carrier II; then X-89 (hard), Duranium, deep blue (Fleet Logistics, Sling Relay, Light/Wave). Dread II "dead". Destroyer II often first tech: R2 Fracture raid of the 2-planet relic system (carrier+4 fighters, 1 infantry each) with 2 Destroyer II + carrier + fighters + 2 infantry wins reliably -> 4 influence + 2 relics. Destroyer II also deters R2 Mecatol contests.
- Fleet: carrier + destroyers ("tugboats") — cheap ships unlock commander; build every destroyer.
- War suns: with Industrex/yellow skip + 6-resource home (free war sun legendary); if Muaat in game with Industrex, pay a lot for their promissory.
- Trading: sell War Funding (promissory) — if nobody buys, sell it cheap anyway; trade fragments/support for Jol-Nar research agreement — tech transformed game.
