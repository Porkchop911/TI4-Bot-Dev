# SCPT 399 Super Guide to the Strategy Phase (2025-04-16, Matt Martens + Hunter; PoK era + Codex) — own transcription base.en
- 0:14 Only choice in strategy phase: pick a card in speaker order; speaker order -> access to initiative. No transactions during strategy phase (deals are non-binding).
- 0:18 Summit (+2 tokens): just play it. Manipulate Investments: if speaker, put 3 TG on the card you'll take; otherwise sprinkle on cards you want others to take (e.g., to sell speaker/politics).
- 0:22 Cards' value shifts over the game; only ~2 stay bottom all game.
- LEADERSHIP (0:22-0:53): spend influence in groups of 3 (order planets influence-low, use 2/1 combos to hit 3/6/9 thresholds; spend planets for their larger stat). Early game weakest overall but: only reliable way to do >2 secondaries R1 (structure objectives / two spend objectives); better for weak-economy factions. Primary = best raw economic deal (3 tokens ~ 9 bucks). But tokens are lowest-priority currency early vs plastic/TG; flips to most important late. Leadership taking R1 leaves you plastic-light. R2: if you are taking custodians, you need Leadership to go first. Mid game: rich factions/stall-heavy factions (Yssaril, Sling Relay, Bio-Stims, component actions) can do a LEADERSHIP STALL — delay playing it until others run out of actions and pass, so they can't buy tokens in time; high risk: they still buy tokens and come back next round with double energy (counter-slay); best version forces someone to score the objective they didn't want to (pushes them onto a blockable control objective for R5). Counter: if stalled, just pass early. Late (R5): Leadership second in win-slay carousel after Imperial; status-phase scorer first; Leadership has no defence except dumping tokens in fleet and building at home.
- 0:48 Token allocation in status phase (Matt): put one in tactic and one in strategy; ensure ~2 strategy tokens so stalls don't lock you out of Imperial+Tech secondaries. Hunter: no fixed heuristic — minimal secondaries needed for visible objectives; budget a strategy token for TRADE secondary (replenish) when others refuse to refresh you (X-2 offers).
- DIPLOMACY (0:53-1:05): PoK version = primary readies 2 planets too. R1: if a spend objective is out and Diplomacy is your only way to score R1, ALWAYS take it (equalizes; removes a "gimme" spend point from late game). Diplomacy rises R1 mainly because it denies WARFARE (warfare gives others more energy); "if you don't need warfare don't take it". Late game: Diplomacy on home system blocks everyone except a Warfare player; third in carousel; arguably best late-game card for defending a status-phase win. Can't target Rex (hosts wish it could). Fleet Logistics + Diplomacy = take and lock same turn.
- POLITICS (1:05-): order: choose speaker first, then draw 2 action cards, then look at top 2 agendas. R1 Politics mostly decides custodians (R2 Leadership first action). Blue-start factions can Gravity Drive R1 and jump Rex R2. Any faction can R1-custodians with Diplomacy+influence in theory.
- POLITICS cont. (1:08): most common R1 play: sell speaker to the player one seat behind (upstream) for cash — they then take Politics later and pass it back, buying you Imperial. Experimental: selling 2-3 seats back. Hunter refuses binding-ish speaker deals (doesn't trust). Mid-game: don't PAY for speaker — the Politics holder assigns speaker for their own benefit; predict who will take Imperial next round. Hunter: never assign speaker to YOURSELF in R2 (one player behind you playing speaker control locks you to 4th-6th pick); policy "don't let the speaker token pass behind me" (stay top 3 picks). If upstream players pass it along, you get Imperial. Hunter placed a PDS on Rex to make attackers' math marginal — marginal deterrence often enough.
- CONSTRUCTION (1:18): primary 2 structures (1 can be dock), secondary 1 structure + locks system (spend token). Weakest card all game ("dead card") except faction needs (Cabal, Sardakk) or structure objectives / great forward dock last pick; may deny Warfare. Late game cards 3-7 roughly equal for back-of-pack players; WARFARE best of those late (stalls, answers Diplomacy lock). Back-of-pack players should coordinate picks (Warfare/Tech). Structure stage II objectives not worth hedging (control-objective-like, blockable); hedge: put docks/PDS on planets essential for control objectives, maybe stack at home.
- TRADE (1:28): best card in the abstract; automatic early first pick; mid-game solid econ; dies late. Keep a strategy token to replenish via secondary when Trade holder offers X-2 or refuses you. Debt meta/"I don't do debt" exists; boat-float logic: rising tide keeps poor factions afloat; making everyone poor only widens rich faction lead (Hacan). Too much money is useless (Xxcha/Jol-Nar >$30 = $20) — bringing Sardakk from 8 to 13 matters. Trade>Diplomacy in flexibility (money keeps; Diplo planets must be spent this round). Trade+next player Politics deal possible.
- WARFARE (1:38): primary remove a token from board (+redistribute); secondary = home dock production (PoK; TE changed to any units with production). Secondary vital for 1-carrier/poor-infantry starts — DON'T take Warfare R1 when Jol-Nar/Nekro/Naalu need it; force them to take it (deny secondary). R1 Warfare is key to some R1 custodians (Hacan, Jol-Nar). Mid-game often unpicked (Leadership covers needs). Late: last in order, only card to answer Diplomacy lock. Don't be mean to L1Z1X: they are the table's slayer — leave them gas.
- 1:47 Matt: at some point flip the coin — attack early rather than stall.
- TECHNOLOGY (1:50): primary ~6 bucks + tech tempo (some factions need ~5 techs; tech out of fashion R5). Stall Technology more (play it only when you need the tech; tech is "new Warfare" to stall). Coordinate R1 timing between Technology, Diplomacy and Warfare holders (tech->diplo->warfare or warfare->diplo->tech; spend-objective Diplo last). Double tech best R3 when economy set. R5: avoid taking Tech (monkey's paw: gives others 3rd unit upgrade / Fleet Logistics / Light/Wave) unless deliberately arming the slayers (e.g. L1).
- IMPERIAL (2:00): never R1. R5: first in order, take it only if you can win in ACTION phase (mix: public + MR point or new secret); for a status-phase win Imperial is worst (last to score — very rarely wins on the 8 in R5 status). Mid-game: Rex point means no secret draw -> secret tempo risk; if Imperial wasn't taken R2, then on R3 take it for public + DRAW SECRET instead of MR point (Matt). Prefer "face-down tempo" (hidden secrets) over "face-up" (visible). Mid-game Imperial (R2/R3) frees you from speaker order: bonus point + slight tempo lead without being the obvious target = platonic ideal; only reliable bonus point = Rex + Imperial.
- 2:20 end of strategy phase: Quantum Datahub Node / Imperial Arbiter swap window.
