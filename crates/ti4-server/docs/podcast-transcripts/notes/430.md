# SCPT 430 Crimson Rebellion Beginner's Guide (2025-11-12, Hunter + Blasto) — base.en, 1:40-2:20
- R1: want a 2nd carrier out (via Sling Relay or Warfare secondary). TECHNOLOGY better for Crimson than for others (Sling Relay = 3 stalls R1; use saved resources to sling a carrier/dread); generally in TE R1 Technology is worse because Warfare usually pops first action (following tech's build impossible).
- Imperial plan (both hosts' default every game): take Imperial R2 or R3, use breaches: destroyer adjacent to Mecatol + start/observe a fight at Rex to place breach, flip with Sarrow, fly fleet from home, commit free mech, play Imperial for public + Rex point, then take Rex's ingress into the Fracture for Styx. "Get above public tempo" (score all 5 stage I + bonus) is meaningful now because Styx exists.
- Commander alliance: gives huge TG; give it to STATIC rich factions (Hacan, L1Z1X, Empyrean (with Dark Pact +50%), Deepwrought) that can't convert money into actionable plastic quickly — NOT to Muaat/Mahact/Saar/Arborec which turn money into position fast. Swap with someone downstream in speaker order. Mentak (Pillage) = worst neighbour; trade alliance for Promise of Protection. If you sit next to Crimson, offer your best to become their alliance partner.
- Tech: DT start (extra DT via Sarrow frontier), AI Dev -> War Suns possible (blue-red synergy), Fighter II (hero synergy), Assault Cannon (same window as hero; immune to Skilled Retreat — good for combat secrets), Fleet Logistics (sneak to Rex late); less need for Light/Wave (breaches). Sundered (yellow faction tech) weak in TE (home-only).
- Keep tech "one off" two-and-two-colors and two-unit-upgrades objectives early. Don't like early tech objectives.
- Control objectives: breaches + mech deploy give sneaky reach ("Rule distant lands"); always keep a mech in reinforcements to deploy; place breaches frivolously; don't remind opponents they can remove breaches (honest explanation once, no help). Stage II "flagship at Rex" great (hero builds flagship).
- Economy objectives: you and your alliance partner both qualify — two-horse race; partner is more "slayable" so table targets them.
- Hosts: Crimson safe A (would be S in PoK / better at 14 points) — later tier list moved to S.
- Etiquette/anti-pattern example: Deepwrought gave commander to another player for coexistence; Hunter would rather skip R1 scoring and take Imperial R2.
