# web2: lobby, single player, the table in a game

Part of `web2/AGENTS.md`: the Patterns and the rules of Screen size and Text there apply here too.

## Lobby

The owner asked for this on 2026-10-12 (`plans/2026-10-12-LOBBY_PLAN.md`; phases 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7 are built). The lobby answers: who is at this table, and how is the game set up?

- **The lobby looks like the game.** It uses the frame of the game (`ShellFrame` in `src/game/shell/`, which `GameShell` uses too): the map that the table will play at the left, the setup and the players in the panel, and one main button in the footer. It is one screen with no dialog and no form. On a phone it is the panes **Map · Players · Setup**, with the footer under every pane.
- **A change applies at once.** A seat that is added or removed, a value of the setup, a name and a faction is sent with the click, and every player sees it at once. There is no "Apply" button. Exception to "a choice is staged, then sent" (the Decisions section of `decisions.md`): the setup of a table is not a decision of the game, nothing of it is hidden, and it can be changed until the start.
- **The same screen for everyone** (pattern 10). A player who is not the host sees the setup with its values and no controls of the table, and is told a change of the host once, as a toast ("The host set the players to 5"). A visitor with no seat sees the same, with the name and "Join" in the footer.
- **A control is where the choice is made** (pattern 1). The kinds of a seat that the server plays are buttons in the row of an open seat ("Rule bot", "Random"); "×" is in the row of the seat that it removes; "Join in a new tab" is in the row of the first open seat, for a player of the table (it joins as another player, "Ada 2", and opens the tab of that seat). Beside the count of the players there is one button for the usual target: "Fill with rule bots" (pattern 8).
- **The models are kinds of a seat** (`mlp:<name>`; `plans/2026-10-15-MLP_INFERENCE_PLAN.md`, section F). Every `mlp:` kind of the options is a button in the row of an open seat, labelled by its model, after "Rule bot" and "Random"; its seat is named by the label and a number ("Reviewer 473312 1"). `KIND_SIGNS` of `select/lobby.ts` matches the prefix. "Fill with rule bots" stays the usual target. A server offers at most three models: the row of an open seat fits them at 1920×1080, and on a phone its buttons wrap under each other, with no short label (`e2e/lobby.spec.ts`, `lobby.phone.spec.ts`, `local.spec.ts`, `local.phone.spec.ts`). Online the server plays the seat; the online lobby and single player are the same rows (`selectLobby`).
- **The setup is five rows, each with its control for the host** (pattern 1): "Players", "Map", "Strategy cards", "Bots wait", "Start from".
  - An amount is a counter in steps of one (pattern 8): the players ("− 6 +"), and the wait in steps of half a second ("Off" at zero). A step that cannot be taken stays in its place, dimmed, and says why on hover ("A person is in every seat": a smaller table drops its open seats first, then the seats that the server plays, never a person).
  - One of a few is a list (the `Select` of `ui/`): the sets of strategy cards, and "Start from" ("Round 1" and the scenarios of the engine, as the Dev sheet of a local game has them). The list is a native control, so it is no dialog and needs no room.
  - What a row's choices are is behind "?": the cards of a set that the usual set does not have, what a scenario holds, what the wait does. The page writes no name of a set, a card, a scenario or a kind of seat: they come from `GET /api/setup-options` (`SetupOptions`), and until the server said them a kind of seat is not offered.
  - A scenario is a game of its own: it has its seats, its map, its cards and its factions, so these rows show its values with no control, and the players sit in the order of the join. A scenario of three seats plays on the seed of the table, and its opening (the answers of the first seat up to the question of the scenario) is played at the start when a person has that seat. "Start from" is a tool for testing and is not hidden: every player reads it. The public server has no scenarios, and no row for them.
- **The name of the viewer is edited in its row** ("✎"): the name opens in place, Enter takes it, Esc or a click elsewhere leaves it.
- **A name is one that the server takes** (`validNickname` of `web-shared/src/identity/nickname.ts`, the server's `storage::validate_nickname`): trimmed, at most 64 bytes of UTF-8, with no control or formatting character. The page sends no other: a name that the server would refuse has a red border, "Join" waits for another, and Enter in the row keeps the name open. The start screen marks it too, and a table is then made under "Player". A name of `?online&name=…` that the server would refuse is left out. "Join in a new tab" cuts a long name to give room to its number. The name of the start screen is kept in `ti4.nickname`, the key of web/ too.
- **The faction is chosen in the row of its seat** (owner, 2026-10-12; phase 3). The faction of the row is the control there ("Hacan ▾", in the place of the word): it opens the choice under the row, in place. While the choice is open, the row is that choice: its other actions ("✎", "×") are hidden. A choice closes it, and so do Esc, a click outside the row and a second click on the faction. On a phone the choice is a sheet over the pane ("Faction of Ada", with its "×"), like a reference sheet: it has the room of the footer and of the tab bar, and a choice, its "×" or Esc closes it. The row of the viewer has it, and for the host a row of a seat that the server plays. A row of another person is not a control, also for the host (pattern 11; the server would allow it).
  - The choice is one list: "Random" first, then the factions in the groups of the server ("Base game", "Prophecy of Kings"; `faction_groups` of the setup options), in three columns, two in the sheet of a phone. A row is the control and the choice applies at once; the chosen row has "▸". The rows have no keys: there are too many.
  - A faction that another seat has stays in its place, is dimmed and says who has it. The server refuses it too (409), and that answer is a toast ("Jol-Nar is already taken by Ada").
  - A seat that is left at "Random" gets a faction that no other seat has, from the seed, at the start.
  - A table that starts from a scenario has the factions of the scenario: no row has the control.
  - A faction has the name that the game calls it by (`factionName`, from the catalogue), in the row and in the list.
- **The sheet of a faction is behind its name**: behind "?" of a row of the choice, and behind the faction of a row that is no control (`Peek`, with the dotted underline). It has the values of the sheet (the home system with `res/inf`, the commodities, the starting technologies, the starting fleet by place) and the faction abilities, the faction technologies and the leaders as printed, in the frame of the cards of a player sheet. It is longer than a window can be high, so it opens on the side of its trigger with more room and scrolls inside (`tall` of `Peek`). Esc closes an open sheet first, then the choice. All of it is the catalogue (`faction_sheets`, `ti4_host::view::components::FactionSheet`); `select/factionSheet.ts` words the lines.
- **A state has one sign, with its word on hover** (patterns 4 and 6), in a column of fixed width: "★" host, "⚙" the rule bot, "⚄" a random seat, "✦" a model (its label on hover: "Reviewer 473312: played by the page"), "◇" not connected, a dashed outline for an open seat. The list has no numbers and no seat colours: the rows are the seats of the game, in their order.
- The faction of a row is in a column of fixed width: the name from the catalogue, or "Random". It is public.
- A name is in a column of fixed width and is cut with "…", with the full name on hover; on a phone it takes a second line (pattern 13). The row of the viewer has "(you)"; another player of this browser has "(yours)", and that row is the control that makes the tab that player.
- The main button is in the footer, and Enter is the main button: "Join" for a visitor, "Start game" for the host. A player who is not the host has none; the note says "Waiting for Ada to start". While the start waits, the note says why ("Waiting for 3 open seats").
- No text that explains the lobby is visible. What the kinds of a seat do is behind "?" beside the heading of the players.
- A call that the server refuses is a toast with the reason, and the lobby stays as it was. A lost connection has one signal, in the header: "Offline · reconnecting". The page tries again by itself.
- The page does not know where the table is kept. `Table` (`src/session/table.ts`) is the seam of the setup, as `Transport` is the seam of a game: the table as it is, the board of the preview, and the calls. `serverTable` has the socket of the table (`/ws/tables/<game_id>`, `crates/ti4-server/src/ws/table.rs`: the lobby view, whole, after every change; a player is connected while its socket is open) and the HTTP calls of the lobby. The calls themselves (the addresses, the header of the player session, `createTable`, the join, the other posts of the lobby, whether a game is gone) are `web-shared/src/net/api.ts`, which web/ calls too; `table.ts` words their refusals. Of two views the one with the higher `lobby_version` is kept. `select/lobby.ts` makes the `LobbyView` (`src/model/lobby.ts`): which rows, signs, buttons and notes a viewer has is decided there, not in the component. When the table says `running`, the page closes this socket and opens the socket of the game.
- The setup of a game is decided in Rust, once, in `ti4-host/src/setup.rs` (`TableSetup`, the options, the preview, the draw of the factions, the game): the server calls it for the table of a lobby, and the engine of the page has the same functions as exports (`ti4_setup_options`, `ti4_setup_preview`, `ti4_setup_draw`, and `ti4_host_start` with the table; `setupOptionsOf`, `setupPreviewOf`, `setupDrawOf` and `LocalGame.table` in `src/session/wasmTransport.ts`). A table of a seed with the same kinds of seats is the same game on both, and every time: a seat that the host plays decides by the seed, its place in the order of the seats and the number of the decision, not by the id of its player (`ti4_host::bots::choose`, for the rule bot, a random seat and an MLP model `mlp:<name>`; `crates/ti4-server/tests/it/both_hosts.rs`, `tests/it/seat_order.rs`). The options list a kind `mlp:<name>` for every model that the host is given: the server its loaded models, `ti4_setup_options` the `models` of its request (`setupOptionsOf(module, players, models)`). A new value of the setup is a field of `TableSetup`, not code of one host.
- The board is the board of the game (`Board`), over the tiles of `GET …/lobby/map-preview`, asked again when `map_revision` changes. A home is the place of a seat: it has no planets and no faction, because nobody sits there before the start.
- There is no "Ready", for any table: the host starts when every seat is filled.
- **The rows are the seats** (owner, 2026-10-15). The players sit in the order of the rows: the order in which they came, until the host shuffles them. "Shuffle seats" is beside the count of the players, for the host before the start (`POST …/lobby/shuffle-seats`; `Table.shuffleSeats`): the rows are put in another order at once, as every change, and a player who is not the host is told once ("The host shuffled the seats"). Nothing of the seats is drawn at the start; the factions of the seats that chose none are drawn from the seed. In single player the shuffle moves the seat of the person too: the page plays the seat where its row is.
- **Single player is the same screen over a table in the page** (phase 6). `?single` is its address (`src/dev/SingleApp.tsx`): with no game of this browser it is the setup, and with one it is that game. The address names the save, not the setup, so a reload of a started game is that game.
  - `localTable` (`src/session/localTable.ts`) is a `Table` that is kept in the page. The engine of the page says what it can be and checks every change (the setup exports), so a change that the engine refuses is a toast, as a refusal of the server is. `TableState.local` says that the table is of the page; `select/lobby.ts` reads it, the screen does not.
  - What differs, each with a reason that the player sees: the title is "Single player · 6 players"; there is no "Copy link" and no "Join in a new tab" (nobody else can come); the name of the player is not edited there (it is the name of the start screen); a kind of seat is "played by the page".
  - The models: `SingleApp` asks `GET /api/models` once (`listModels` of `src/session/models.ts`), with the engine, and `localTable` passes them to `ti4_setup_options`. With no server, or no model, there is no MLP kind. When a seat gets an MLP kind, the table loads its file into the Cache API (`modelFile`), and "Start game" waits until the files of the table are loaded: the footer says how far ("Loading Reviewer 473312 · 12 / 23 MB", `TableState.loading`), as it says why a start waits. A file that does not load is a toast, and its seats are open again.
  - The table begins with a rule bot in every other seat, and a seat that the count adds is a rule bot: nobody can join, so an open seat is only the step between two kinds ("×", then "Rule bot" or "Random" in the row). A new table has the seats that this browser chose last, else six. `?single&seed=3&players=4` names the seed and the seats of a new table, for a test.
  - "Bots wait" is the wait of the page: the engine asks before each decision of a seat that the page plays, but not before a reaction window that the seat only declines (`stepping` 2 of the hosted game), and the transport waits before it lets the seat decide (`LocalPlay.waitMs`). The page is shown the game while it waits, so the toolbar says "Waiting for Rule bot 2". The game is the same game with every wait, and a game that is played again does not wait for its saved decisions: a reload while a seat takes its time goes on at that seat. "Other seats: Step" of the Dev sheet comes first: with it the seats wait for the button.
  - "Start game" makes the game, and the page is the game (`LocalApp`, with "Start" as the way out). It is kept from its first question on, in the save store under the slot `single` (the save of the engine names its table; the wait is kept next to it), so `?single` opens it (the saved game in `local-play.md`). "New game" of its Dev sheet drops the game and opens the setup. The Dev sheet of such a game has no "Start from": the setup has that row.
- What a `Table` promises is tested once for both tables (`src/session/tableContract.test.ts`): the table in the page, and the table of a lobby on a real server.
- The seam has the maps too (`Table.maps`, `Table.setMap`), for phase 4. No screen has a control for the map yet.
- **The table in the game** (phase 7, 2026-10-14). The sheet "Table" answers the question of the lobby for a game that runs: who is at this table, and how was the game set up?
  - A game says who sits in each seat: `seats` of the snapshot and of the update, by player id (`ti4_host::view::seats::SeatFact`: the nickname and who plays the seat). It is public. The server takes it from the record of the table, the engine of the page from the table that the game was started from (`TableSeat.name`). A game with no table (`?local=…`, `?scenario=…`) has none, and its seats keep their letters ("Seat A").
  - **A seat is named by its player** wherever the page says who a seat is: the name column of the player table (fixed width, cut with "…", pattern 13), the title of the sheet of a player, the status of the toolbar ("Waiting for Ada", "Turn of Rule bot 1"), the list of the seats of a strategic action (`SeatView.name`, and `seatLabel` in `select/names.ts`). A text that states the game keeps the faction ("After Hacan’s tactical action"): it is the wording of a card or of an event.
  - A seat that no person plays has its sign in the player table, in a column of its own: "⚙" the rule bot, "⚄" a random seat, "✦" a model, as in the lobby, with the word on hover (`SeatView.played`; the label of a model is its seat's name without the number). A table of people only has no such column.
  - The sheet is the last reference sheet ("Table"), in a game that has a table. It is the lobby with nothing open (pattern 10; `TableSheet` over the same `LobbyView`): "Players" has the seats in their order, each with the symbol of its seat, its sign, the player and the faction (its sheet behind the name); "Setup" has the rows of the lobby with their values. `select/lobby.ts` decides it from the phase of the table: after the start no row has a control, no name and no faction is edited, and no seat is removed.
  - **The wait is the one value that changes after the start**: the host has its counter in the row "Bots wait" of the sheet, where the lobby has it. Online it is `POST /api/games/{id}/settings` (host only; the record of the table keeps it, so a recovered game has it); in single player it is the capability `wait` of the transport, and the saved game keeps it. A seat that waits now finishes its wait.
  - The sheet reads the table while it is in view (`src/dev/GameTable.tsx`): the socket of the table online, `localGameTable` (`src/session/localTable.ts`) in single player. A call that the table refuses is a toast.
  - **A visitor of a running game is shown the table first** (decision 14): `?online=<game_id>` with no player of this browser is the lobby screen of the running table, with its board, "Watch" as the main button (Enter) and no name to give. "Watch" opens the game with no seat. A tab with no seat in a game where the browser has several players is that screen too, with "(yours)" on their rows.
  - "Rejoin" is in the row of a seat whose person is away for longer than the grace time of the server (`can_take_over`), for a visitor only, on that screen and in the sheet of a visitor who watches. It takes the seat over under the name that it has; the browser keeps the player, and the page is loaded as that player. The server has the last word, and its refusal is a toast. The button does not repeat the name of its row (a name can be long); the name is in its tooltip.
- Not there yet (the later phases of the plan): the choice of the map.
