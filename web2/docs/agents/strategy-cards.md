# web2: strategy cards

Part of `web2/AGENTS.md`: the Patterns and the rules of Screen size and Text there apply here too.

## Strategy cards

All eight cards use one frame: one list of seats. There are no tabs, no seat column and no trail.

- The list has the primary ("P") and then the secondaries in seat order. Each seat that did something or decides, and the seat of the viewer, is one full-width line: order, symbol, name, state, and what the seat did. All decisions made so far are on one screen, with no click.
- The other seats that wait are one line under the list, "Then: Blair, Cora", in their order (`waitingInShort` in `select/strategy.ts`); the same under the band of an ability that asks in the middle of the action.
- A state has a sign: "▶ Deciding", "✎ Private draft", "✎ Draft ready", "✓ Resolved", "✓ Followed", "– Passed"; the row of the viewer while it waits, "· Waits for Blair".
- Only the row of the viewer opens: into the editor, in place, while the viewer has a choice or an open draft there. After it resolves the row closes and shows its result like any other row. A ready draft is closed and says what it will do.
- Other rows are not controls. A choice of another player is visible when it is resolved, never before.
- The list and "Then: …" inform (`BlockRole`, `log.md`): they are not in the step sheet but in the sheet of the turn that its row of the Log opens. While the viewer decides, the step sheet has only the row of the viewer, open into its editor (a list of one row); with no decision of the viewer open there is no step sheet.
- In a strategic turn that the Log opens, every row says what its seat did in short, and opens with a click ("▸"/"▾") to its lines when they say more (`log.md`, "A turn, opened"). In Live a row has no details: the viewer's row opens into its editor.
- The list is dense (24px rows) so that the largest editor, the technology tree with a payment, fits with all eight seats.

- The whole primary is one screen. Every part is staged; "Resolve primary" sends all of it. It is split only where the game reveals hidden information (Politics: the agenda cards are a second screen, sent again).
- A part of a card is a block that uses one of the decision shapes. The board and the player table serve the first part that is not complete.
- A chosen thing is a row with "Change". "Change" clears that part, so it is the open part again. Esc clears the last choice, then leaves the action.
- A part that needs no choice is a value: "Trade goods 2 → 5", "Victory points 6 → 7".
- A secondary is the same editor as its part of the primary, in the row of the viewer: the pill of the heading says "Private draft"; while the game asks, the status of the toolbar says "Your decision" and the heading has no pill. It can be drafted before the seat is reached. It states what it costs: "Strategy pool 2 → 1", or who gave it for free.
- A secondary with a choice: nothing chosen is a pass, and the main button says so. A secondary without a choice (Politics, Trade, Imperial) is a list with "Follow" and "Pass", keys 1 and 2.
- In a game of the engine (2026-10-12) the list of the seats is built, with the facts of the engine (`secondary` of the update): the names are the players of the table (the factions in a game with no table), and the viewer is "You". The row of the viewer opens into the editor of its question (below), or into the question of the game as a list where no editor has its shape, with the question and "Strategy pool 2 → 1" above it. A row of another seat has its state and no text of what the seat did.
- The draft of a secondary is in Live, not in the Draft tab: it is the row of the viewer. It opens by itself from the moment the card is played (owner, 2026-10-12), also while the primary is resolved; Trade opens with its window, because the primary changes what the secondary costs. Each answer is recorded with "Record". When the draft has nothing more to decide, the main button is "Mark draft ready". A ready draft has "Change draft"; "Start over" drops the answers.
- A ready draft is sent when the game asks the seat, and the seat is not asked. The game checks it first: a draft that no longer fits is not ready, the footer says so, and the seat decides in Live. The game sends it, not the page: the server online (the Online play section of `online.md`), the engine in a local game. A draft that is not ready when the seat is reached is not sent: the game asks, and "Send draft" sends the draft as it is.
- A secondary that asks the seat nothing as the game is (Leadership with no influence to spend) is no draft: the row says "Nothing to decide as the game is now".
- A draft ends where the game would show something hidden (the cards of Politics, the objective of Imperial). The row says so, and the draft can be ready: the game goes on from there in Live.
- A command pool is one line, on desktop and on a phone: the pool sign and its name, the count ("3 → 5") in a fixed width that never wraps, then a triangle for each token, about the size of the text, close together; only the tokens wrap, in their own column. The fleet pool points up, the tactic and strategy pools point down, as on the command sheet. A token that stays is filled, an added token is filled in the accent on a tint, a removed token is an outline with "−". The tokens are the control, as the tokens of a production are, and there is no counter: a tap on an added token takes it back, a dashed slot with "+" of the size of a token adds one while there is one to place, and a removed token comes back with a tap. A token that the pool keeps (the least that the game offers) is no control, and a token that stays can be taken only while none is added. Padding, not the size of the glyph, makes a token big enough for a finger.
- On a phone every decision of the pools of the viewer opens on the Map pane, with its blocks in the step sheet over the map (`EditorView.onMap`, `BoardView.blocks`): the gain and the purchase of Leadership (primary and secondary) and of Warfare, the redistribution of the status phase and of Thunder's Edge's Warfare, and the recall of Warfare with its system on the map. Desktop has them in the step sheet over the board as well.
- The details that a first question gives about the later ones (the look-ahead of a card) are a struct of the engine, next to the code that builds it, written with `Choice::hinted` and generated with the wire types (`ti4_wasm::wire_ts`); an editor reads them with `hintOf` of `select/editor.ts` and the generated type, not with casts. The payment of a research and of a purchase share `PaymentHints` (the cost, `planets`, `trade_goods`, `trade_good_worth`, `commodities`).
- In a game of the engine every screen of several questions below (Leadership's purchase, the readying of Diplomacy, the agenda deck of Politics, the structures of Construction, the replenish of Trade, the recall of Warfare, the score of Imperial) is an editor of `select/editors.ts`: it is chosen by the kind and the details of the question, what it stages is one slot of the page (`LocalState.editor`), and its main button sends its answers by id, with a plan where the game asks a payment, in one request (`Transport.applyDraft`). A new editor is an entry there and in its own module, not a branch of `useLiveSession.ts`.
- In a game of the engine (2026-10-16) every redistribution of command tokens (`mode: "redistribute"`: the status phase with its gained tokens, before and after Thunder's Edge's Warfare) is the pool editor, not a list (`select/tokens.ts`). The staging starts at the sheet of now, and the header says "<placed> / <total> placed"; the footer says what is left to place, then the change ("Tactic 2 → 3 · Fleet 3 → 4"). The bounds of each counter come from the arrangements that the game offers (the least count of that pool among them, and the tokens left), not from a rule; "Send" waits for an offered arrangement. Esc puts the pools back. A restack (`mode: "restack"`, Predictive Intelligence at the end of a turn), which the game asks one token at a time (options of kind `redistribute`, "<from>|<to>", and a decline that ends it), is the same pool editor (`select/restack.ts`): every arrangement of the tokens of the sheet, and the main button ("Redistribute", "Skip" while the pools are those of now) answers one move for each token, from a pool that shrinks to a pool that grows, then the decline, in one request (`Transport.applyDraft`).
- In a game of the engine (2026-10-16) a gain of command tokens and the purchase of tokens for influence are one screen (`select/purchase.ts`): every question with `kind: "command_tokens"` and mode "gain" (Leadership's three tokens; Warfare's one when the game asks it apart from its recall), and the purchase question of Leadership (mode "buy" with `details.purchase`, the engine's `PurchaseHints`), which is also the window of its secondary. "Buy command tokens" is a counter up to `purchase.max`, at `purchase.cost` influence each; "Command pools" places the gained and the bought tokens from the sheet of now ("3 gained · 2 bought · 4 / 5 placed"). On a phone "Buy command tokens" and "Command pools" are in the step sheet over the map, with the payment bar above it, so that buying, placing and paying need no other pane. While tokens are bought the map is the payment, as in a production: the planets of `purchase.planets` with their worth, the trade goods in the payment bar, Auto-pay and "Clear payment", and the payment line in the footer. The main button is "Buy 2 tokens" (or "Buy nothing" for a purchase question, "Send" for a gain); it waits until every token is placed and the payment covers the cost. It sends one plan (`pool`, `purchase` and `pay` steps of `PlanStep`): the game takes a pool for each gained token, "yes" and a pool for each bought token, then "no", and a payment from the staged ones whenever it asks for one (`plan_answer` in `ti4-host/src/planning/steps.rs`). Esc clears the whole screen. In the draft of a secondary the purchase stays a list, one answer at a time.
- In a game of the engine (2026-10-16) a readying of exhausted planets is one screen (`select/ready.ts`): every question with `details.ready_hints` and `details.ready_count` (the engine's `ReadyHints`, whose fields are top-level keys of the details), which is the system question of Diplomacy's primary (options of kind `system`), the window of its secondary (`yes` and `no`), and a question of one planet to ready (options of kind `ready`). "System" (the primary only) is chosen on the map from the systems of the options, with "Change" and who places a command token there; then "Ready planets" ("1 / 2") is chosen on the map from the planets of `ready_hints`, each a row "Exhausted → ready · 4 resources / 2 influence" with "Change". On a phone the whole screen is on the Map pane (`EditorView.onMap`, `BoardView.blocks`): "System" and "Ready planets" are in the step sheet over the map where they are chosen, so that the system, the planets and the main button need no other pane; desktop has them in the step sheet over the board as well. No more planets are taken than the game readies (`ready_count`, or fewer when fewer are exhausted), and the main button waits for that many, since the game offers no decline. A note says which cards that spend resources or influence were played this round and which are to come (`ready_hints.spenders`). The window of the secondary has the cost in the header of the block ("Strategy pool 2 → 1"); nothing chosen is "Pass", else "Follow · ready 2 planets". The main button ("Resolve primary") answers the system or the window and each planet, by the ids of their options, in one request (`Transport.applyDraft`). Esc clears the last choice: the last planet, then the system. In the draft of a secondary it stays a list, one answer at a time.
- In a game of the engine (2026-10-16) Politics' primary is two screens, because the agenda cards are revealed after the first. The first is the question with options of kind `speaker` and `details.seats`: "Speaker" is chosen in the player table ("Speaker: Sol → Letnev"), and "Action cards" is the value of `details.draws` ("Action cards 0 → 2"); `details.draws` and `details.looks` are the engine's `PoliticsHints`, read with `hintFieldsOf`; while `details.looks` is more than 0 the main button is "Send · look at the agenda deck". The second is every question with the options `top` and `bottom` and `details.agendas` (the engine's `AgendaPlacementHints`, `select/agendaDeck.ts`): the first question names every agenda to place, in the order that the game asks. Each agenda is a source block as the catalogue prints it (`Catalog.components.agendas`), with "Top" and "Bottom" as a segmented toggle on the line of its name (the `choices` of the source block; keys 1 to 4, the chosen part with "✓"). "Agenda deck after" is shown when every agenda is placed, as the confirmation: the deck as the game builds it, an agenda placed on top over the agendas placed on top before it, one on the bottom under those before it. There is no "Swap": the game offers no other order. The main button ("Place 2 agenda cards") waits until every agenda is placed and answers each question by the id of its option in one request (`Transport.applyDraft`). A choice made again takes it back; Esc clears the last place. Only the seat of the primary is asked these questions, so no other seat sees the agendas. The secondary is the question of the game as a list ("Decline", "Draw").
- In a game of the engine (2026-10-16) a placement of structures is one screen (`select/structures.ts`): every question with `details.structures` (the engine's `StructureHints`), which is the first placement of Construction's primary, the choice of Thunder's Edge's ability (`structure` and `produce|<system>`), the window of the secondary (`yes` and `no`), and a lone placement. The details have every placement from this question on, each with its options (a kind on a planet of a system), every kind on every other planet that the player controls with the first check of the engine that excluded it (`withheld`, `StructureWithheld`: a space station, PDS only, Demilitarized Zone, the limit of the planet, a ban of an ability, none left in the reinforcements), and what a chosen option of the placement before takes away (a second PDS where the first fills the planet), the player's structures on each planet, and the PDS and space docks on the board against the box. Gauges show "Space docks on the board" and "PDS on the board" with what is staged. "Structures" has a row for each placement ("1 of 2 · space dock or PDS", "2 of 2 · PDS only" as the engine asks), chosen as "Qucen'n · #29 · PDS 1 → 2" with "Change", which opens that placement again with the planets of its system. The whole choice is on the map (owner, 2026-10-16), and the map stays the choice while a structure is open or staged: the systems where the open placement has an option, and its planets ("Place a structure on …"; a planet with a staged structure has the check mark). A click on such a system chooses it: "System" is a row with "Change" (a click on another system chooses that one, "Change" clears it), and the panel lists every planet of that system that the player controls, one group for each planet with a row for "Space dock" and one for "PDS" (keys after the ability), titled "Structure 1 of 2 · space dock or PDS". A row that the open placement offers has "1 → 2" and places it; a staged row is selected and takes it back; a row that the open placement does not offer stays in view, dimmed, with why: "Not after the PDS on Qucen'n" (taken away by the staged placement before), "Every structure is placed", else the engine's reason: "Space station", "PDS only", "Demilitarized Zone", "Has a space dock" or "Has 2 PDS", "Barred", "None left". The client makes no reason of its own beyond what is staged. A click on a planet of the map acts on the open placement: a planet with a staged structure takes it back (the last one there); a planet where the open placement offers one structure places it, in any system, and its system is chosen (the second PDS, or a PDS where the planet has a space dock); a planet where it offers both kinds chooses its system, whose list has both. A second structure on the same planet is placed from the list. After a placement the system stays while the next placement has an option there; else the map chooses the system of the next one. On a phone the choice stays on the Map pane: the blocks are in the step sheet over the map (`EditorView.onMap`, as Leadership's purchase), and once a system is chosen the inspector of that system does not cover them (`Board`). Desktop has the blocks in the step sheet over the board as well, and a click on a system also opens its inspector. Thunder's Edge has "Ability" first: "Place a structure" places two structures of any kind; "Use PRODUCTION · <system>" is sent alone, and its second structure is the screen again after the production. The game offers a decline of each structure, so the main button sends what is staged ("Resolve primary · 1 structure"); a placement with no option is not asked. The window of the secondary has the cost in the header of "Structure"; nothing chosen is "Pass", else "Follow · place PDS". The main button answers the ability or the window and each structure, by the ids of their options, in one request (`Transport.applyDraft`). Esc clears the last structure, then the system, then the ability. In the draft of a secondary it stays a list, one answer at a time.
- In a game of the engine (2026-10-16) a replenish of commodities is one screen (`select/replenish.ts`): every question with `details.commodities`, which is the question of the other seats of Trade's primary (options of kind `replenish` with `details.seats`, and a decline), and the window of its secondary (`yes` and `no`). The primary has "Your seat" with what the card already gave (`details.own`: "Trade goods 4 → 7", "Commodities 1 → 4"; with `details.commodities` of the seats, the engine's `TradeReplenishHints`), and "Other players" ("1 / 2 chosen"): the seats are chosen in the player table, several at once, each row with what it gives that seat ("Commodities 0 → 6"), and each chosen seat is a row with "Change". Only the seats that the game offers can be chosen (those below their commodity limit). The game offers a decline, so nothing chosen is "Resolve primary · nobody else". The window of the secondary is "Replenish commodities" (with "Commodities 0 → 6" from `details.commodities`, the engine's `CommodityStanding`, and what it costs: "Strategy pool 2 → 1", or "no strategy token" when the question says so) and "Pass", keys 1 and 2; the main button waits for one of them. The main button answers each chosen seat, in the order of the options, and then the decline when a seat is left (or the window), by the ids of their options, in one request (`Transport.applyDraft`). Esc clears the last seat, or the answer of the window. In the draft of a secondary it stays a list, one answer at a time.
- In a game of the engine (2026-10-16) a recall of a command token from the board is one screen (`select/recall.ts`): every question with options of kind `recall`, which is Warfare's primary of the Prophecy of Kings, the base game and Codex I (`pok6warfare`). When the question says what comes after it (`details.redistribution`, the engine's `RedistributionHints`: the sheet of now, the gained tokens, the total, the option of the gain into each pool and every arrangement that the redistribution offers), the gain and the redistribution are on the screen too. "Command token" is chosen on the map from the systems of the options, a row "Qucenn/Rarron · #29 · The token returns to your reinforcements · you gain 1 command token" with "Change"; "Command pools" is the pool editor (`select/tokens.ts`) from the sheet of now, with the gained token to place ("8 / 9 placed"), and its bounds from the offered arrangements. The main button ("Resolve primary") waits for the system and an offered arrangement, and answers the system, the gain into a pool that the arrangement fills and the arrangement, by the ids of their options, in one request (`Transport.applyDraft`). Esc puts the pools back, then the system.
- Warfare's secondary (a production in the home system) is the window of the game as a list in the row of the seat ("Decline", "Produce", with "Strategy pool 2 → 1"), then the production screen of a tactical action (the Production section of `tactical.md`): the build at the home system, the payment on the map, sent as one plan. A production outside a tactical action (no active system) is in the frame of its strategic action, with no steps of a tactical action, the card as its heading ("Warfare", beside it "Strategic action · <system> · <faction>") and its printed text behind "?"; so is the PRODUCTION of Thunder's Edge's Construction. The production does not open in the row of the seat: the game knows the build only after the window (`TacticalFacts::Production` comes with the question of the first unit).
- In a game of the engine (2026-10-16) Thunder's Edge's Warfare (`te6warfare`) is what the engine asks, in the screens that exist: the redistribution before the action is the pool editor; the choice of its system is the activation step of a tactical action (`TacticalFacts::Activation` with `costs_token: false`), which offers every system of the board, also one with the seat's command token, and says "No command token · Choose on the map"; the tactical action goes on in Live on the screens of its steps, with "Warfare" in the title of the frame and its primary and secondary behind "?"; the redistribution after it is the pool editor again. A resolved Warfare of Thunder's Edge opened from the Log has the card as its first tab and the steps of its tactical action after it (`log.md`, "A turn, opened").
- In a game of the engine (2026-10-16) a score of a public objective with what follows it is one screen (`select/scoring.ts`): every question with `details.secrets_held` and `details.secrets_max` (the engine's `ImperialHints`, whose fields are top-level keys of the details, read with `hintFieldsOf`), which is the question of Imperial's primary (options of kind `objective`, a decline and `details.controls_mecatol`) and the window of its secondary (`yes` and `no`). "Public objectives" lists the objectives of the options, keys 1 and on, each with its name and printed text from `Catalog.records.public_objectives` and "Victory points 2 → 3" from its printed worth; only the objectives that the game offers are listed. "Then" is a value: "Gain 1 victory point · You control Mecatol Rex · Victory points 3 → 4" (counting the staged objective), else "Draw 1 secret objective · You do not control Mecatol Rex · Secret objectives 1 → 2 of 3". A choice made again takes it back; Esc clears it. The main button is "Resolve primary", or "Resolve primary · score nothing", which sends the decline; it answers by the id of the option (`Transport.applyDraft`). The window of the secondary is "Draw 1 secret objective" (with "Secret objectives 1 → 2 of 3" and what it costs) and "Pass", keys 1 and 2, as Trade's (`select/follow.ts`); the main button waits for one of them. In the draft of a secondary it stays a list, one answer at a time.
- The printed text of a strategy card of the engine is behind "?" of the heading: the primary and the secondary of that card from `Catalog.records.strategy_cards` (`strategyCardHelp` of `select/printed.ts`), so each set of strategy cards has its own text. Wherever a card is shown with its text, both parts are there, each with its title ("Trade · Primary", "Trade · Secondary"): the heading of its strategic action and of a production or a tactical action of the card, each row of the choice of the strategy phase (its "?"), and the source of a reaction window to its strategic action (a source block for each part).
- Exception to "the source is on top": the printed text of a strategy card is behind hover. There are eight cards and every player knows them.
- Several players are chosen in the player table too (Trade). A row shows what the choice gives that player ("Commodities 0 → 6").
- The technology tree is a block in the panel, not a dialog: four colour columns, a row for the unit upgrades, and a row for the faction technologies when the faction has any that are not unit upgrades. A faction technology shows its colour as a square mark before the name. A cell is the control. A filled symbol is a prerequisite that the player has, a hollow one is missing. The printed text of a technology is behind "?".
- Exception to "a chosen thing is a row with Change": in the technology tree the selected cell is the chosen thing and the control. The header of the tree names what is chosen and its price.
- Exception to "keys 1, 2, 3": the cells of the technology tree have no keys. There are too many. Enter and Esc act as everywhere.
- In a game of the engine (2026-10-15) every question that researches a technology is the tree: the primary, the secondary and its draft, and any other card that asks for a research (`select/research.ts`). The game offers what can be researched (the options of kind `research`); a cell of another technology is not a control. The tree around them is printed: `research` of the update (`ti4-host/src/view/research.rs`) has the colour, the prerequisites and the owner of each technology of the seat, and its ready planets with a specialty, which the header of a column names ("Green 1 · +1 Rigel III"). A filled prerequisite mark counts the owned technologies of its colour and these planets; whether a cell can be chosen is the engine's answer. One question is one research: the second research of the primary is the tree again, with its price. A research that may be declined has "Research nothing" as the main button while no cell is chosen.
- In a game of the engine (2026-10-16) a paid research (Technology's secondary at 4 resources, the second research of the primary at 6, `details.payment` of the question, the engine's `ResearchPaymentHints`) is paid on the map, as a production is: once a cell is chosen, the planets of `payment.planets` with their worth are on the map, the trade goods in the payment bar with "Pay rest", Auto-pay and "Clear payment", and the payment line in the footer. The payment bar has the three states of an amount against a cost (short blocks the main button; more than the price is "⚠ 1 wasted" and allowed). The footer says what changes: "Selected: Sarween Tools · Technologies 3 → 4 · Trade goods 4 → 2". A specialty planet that the prerequisites of the chosen technology exhaust (`payment.kept`) does not pay for it: the engine keeps it ready and offers only the technologies that can be paid without it; a specialty of an exhausted planet stands for nothing (the research fact lists the ready ones). The main button sends the research by the id of its option and the payment as `pay` steps of a plan, in one request (`Transport.applyDraft`); the game takes them as it asks, one planet or trade good at a time, and takes a lone way to pay by itself (`plan_answer` in `ti4-host/src/planning/steps.rs`). Esc clears the technology and its payment. In the draft of a secondary the tree records the research alone, and the payment questions that follow are a list, one answer at a time. The window of Technology's secondary stays the list of the game ("Decline", "Spend", with "Strategy pool 2 → 1"): the research is asked after it.
