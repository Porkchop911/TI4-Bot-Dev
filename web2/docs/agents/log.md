# web2: the Log

Part of `web2/AGENTS.md`: the Patterns and the rules of Screen size and Text there apply here too.

## What it is for

- The Log answers one question: what are the others doing, and what did they do, so that I can decide. The decision of the viewer is the step sheet on the map (or a seat in the player table); the Log only reads (owner, 2026-10-16).
- Desktop has it in the right column under the player table, where it fills the column and scrolls inside itself. A phone has it as the pane Log. Both show the same Log (`src/game/log/LogPanel.tsx`).
- The Log is not an event log (owner, 2026-10-17): it is at most about eight large rows, the turns since the viewer's last turn and the phases in between, the turn that is played now last. A row says who did what and how it ended. What happened in a turn is the sheet of its action, read-only, through the same UI as the live game (a tactical action with its steps and the battle table, a strategic card with its seats, a card with its decision); a row opens it from L4 of the plan on. Until then a row is no control.

## The rows

- The rows are oldest first (`src/game/log/LogHistory.tsx`). A turn is a card of three lines: the seat (its symbol, the name of the player and the faction, cut with "…", the full name on hover; pattern 13), "You" on a turn of the viewer, and "Round 1 · turn 5" at the right; then the action ("Tactical · Mecatol Rex · #18", "Strategic · Warfare", "Component · Sabotage", "Pass"); then the summary of at most three outcomes, muted, in at most two lines ("won the space combat · took Meer from Hacan", "3 followed"). There are no dice in a row.
- A strategy, status or agenda phase is a row of its own: its name and its summary ("7 strategy cards picked", "2 objectives scored · revealed Corner the Market", "Mutiny: For"). The start of an action phase has no row: the turns say their round.
- The turn or the phase that is played now is the last row, with "▶" and the frame and tint of the accent (the sign carries the state, pattern 4).
- The Log scrolls inside itself, on desktop in the column under the player table and on a phone as the pane Log. The newest is at the end: the Log opens at its end, and follows what is new while it is there (within 24px of the end); scrolled up, it stays where the viewer is.
- Nothing in the Log is a control (patterns 10 and 11). There is no filter of the seats: a row says its seat, and the Log is short.
- The current action is not in the Log: while the viewer decides it is the step sheet; what another seat does now is its live row.

## Decide and inform

- Each block of the view model has a role (`BlockRole` of `src/model/action.ts`, set in `session/select`, never in a component): "decide" is what the viewer needs for the choice that is open now, "inform" is what happened or what the other seats do. A block without a role decides. The step sheet shows only what decides (owner, 2026-10-16: the map keeps the decisions, not what happens).
  - Informs: the list of the seats of a strategic action and "Then: …"; the turn of another seat, a wait, the end of the game; a combat that the viewer watches (`TaskView.role`).
  - Decides: the row of the viewer with its editor in a strategic action; the picker, its staged action and End turn; every editor and list of a question; the source that asks and its printed text; the action that a window reacts to; the band of a window or of an ability; the steps of a tactical action that the viewer stages; a space combat in which the viewer is asked; the notes of a draft, an error, a lost connection or a replay.
  - What informs is shown again when a row of the Log opens the sheet of its action (L4–L7 of the plan). In Live a resolved step of a tactical action is no tab that opens (`StepTabView.locked`, "in the Log" on hover); a draft keeps its tabs.

## The history

- The history is made from the public events of the game (`GameEvent`), which the transport keeps next to the rolls: the events of a snapshot replace the list, since an undo takes the later ones back, and each `event` message adds one, once by its `id` (`src/session/events.ts`). Both kinds of game get them the same way: the engine of a local game sends the event messages and the snapshot with its events as the server does. An event of a seat or of the referee is not kept. `selectLog` (`src/session/select/log.ts`) makes `ShellView.log` (`LogView`, `src/model/log.ts`) from them. `LogPanel` shows it (`LogHistory`, the section "The rows").
- Only the typed facts of an event are read (`fact`, `roll`), never its `detail` text. An event without a fact adds nothing, and a fact that the Log does not know yet (a newer host) is skipped. Every id is named as the rest of the page names it: a seat by `seatLabel`, a system as "Mecatol Rex · #18", a planet by the board, a unit by its card ("Carrier I"), a technology, a strategy card, an objective, an action card, a leader, a relic, an ability by the catalogue. No raw id is on the screen; without the catalogue a name is the id in words.

### The turn model

- A turn is the events of one `action_id`. It starts at the fact `action_started` (its action: tactical, strategic with its card, component with its source, pass) and ends at `turn_ended`, a pass, the next `action_id`, a phase transition or the end of the game. The rolls of the action phase carry their `action_id` and belong to their turn. A component action within a turn (an agent) is a line of that turn.
- What a seat does at its turn menu before it chooses its action (a transaction, a component action that does not end the turn, such as an agent) has an `action_id` of its own: it is a part of the turn that follows when that turn is of the same seat; else it is a turn of its own, "Transaction". A part that the seat ended (the fact of the end of its turn) is a turn of its own. The turn keeps the id of its first part. Its action is its tactical or strategic action or its pass, else its first component action; another component action in it is a line ("Component action · Carth of Golden Sands").
- The turns are numbered in their round from 1, in the order of the game.
- A phase that is no action phase is one entry, "Strategy phase · Round 3", with what the seats did in it: the picks, the scored objectives, the votes of each agenda, the cards that were played. The start of an action phase is an entry with no details: a divider between the turns.

### The scope

- The Log starts at the last turn of the viewer, and that turn is its first entry; a reaction, a secondary or a vote of the viewer is no turn and does not cut it, so that the player sees what it did and then what the others did after it. Oldest first; the phases in between are their entries. The turn that is played now is last and marked live; a turn that ended with no fact of its end (a tactical action that ends after its movement) is not live once another seat has the turn (`active_player`). A turn of the viewer that is played now is not its last turn: the Log starts at the turn before it.
- A spectator, and a seat before its first turn, see the round in play.

### An entry

- A turn has its seat, its kind ("Tactical · Mecatol Rex · #18", "Strategic · Technology", "Component · Sabotage", "Pass"), its round and its number, a summary of at most three outcomes of its seat ("moved 3 ships · won the space combat · took Meer from Hacan", "researched Gravity Drive"), and its details in groups. A pass has no summary and no details.
- The groups of a tactical or a component action are its steps: Activation, Movement (the ships from each system to each system, by unit, and what was loaded), Space cannon, Space combat, Invasion (the bombardment, the landings by planet), Ground combat, Exploration, Production (what was produced where, and what paid for it), then Effects, Cards played and Transactions. A strategic action has Primary (what its seat did: the research, the structures, the tokens, the system of Diplomacy and the planets it readied, the speaker of Politics, the seats whose commodities Trade replenished, Warfare's recalled token and command sheet) and Secondaries, then Cards played and Transactions. Secondaries has a part for each seat that answered: its answer first ("Followed" or "Declined"), then what it did, in the order of the answers; the seats that declined are last. What a seat does after the end of the turn (a unit removed over its fleet supply) is still a part of it.
- The summary of a strategic action ends with who followed: "2 followed", "none followed". A purchase of a token with influence is its payment ("Spent influence: Jord") and the tokens it gains, with no line of its own; the tokens of a seat are one line ("Gained 2 tactic tokens, 1 fleet token").
- The status phase has a group "Scored", a group "Revealed" with the public objectives that it revealed, and a group "Command tokens" with the command sheet of each seat after its redistribution ("3 tactic · 3 fleet · 2 strategy"); its summary is "2 objectives scored · revealed Corner the Market". The agendas come before the cards played.
- A line has its seat, a text in words, and the dice of a roll (the faces, the face that hits, the hits). Units of one line are counted: "Dreadnought I, 2 Carrier I".

### What the state tells

- Besides the decisions the host tells what changed with no decision, as an event `state_changed` with its fact (`event.actor` is its seat; it has the `action_id` of its action, or the phase of its entry). The Log reads it as it reads a decision: a unit that the game destroyed (`destroyed`, with its count), the end of a combat (`combat_ended`), a planet taken (`control_gained`), an exploration (`explored`), an agenda resolved (`agenda_resolved`), a public objective revealed (`objective_revealed`) and an action card that no decision told (`card_played`).
- A combat is a group of its own: "Space combat · Mecatol Rex · #18 · Jol-Nar vs Hacan", "Ground combat · Meer · Jol-Nar vs Hacan" (the attacker first; under way, the sides are the seats that rolled). It has, in the order of the game: the anti-fighter barrage, a roll in the middle of it (an ability, a card), an announced retreat, each round ("Round 1", the dice of each seat under each other), the losses of each seat in each round ("Lost: Carrier I, 2 Fighter I"), a sustained damage, and its end: "Retreated to …" (or "Retreated" when no decision told where), "Won by Jol-Nar", or "Both sides destroyed". A loss that a seat chose (`casualty`) and one that the game took (`destroyed`) are one line: the host tells each loss once.
- The fact of its end closes a combat: a roll after it starts the next combat. The rounds of a space combat are not told by the host: a roll after a decision or a change of the state, or a second roll of the same seat at the same value (the engine rolls the units of one seat and one value together), starts the next round. A round of ground combat is a fact of the host (`ground_combat_round`, with its planet); a further ground combat on the planet (`ground_combat_started`) is a group of its own, "Started a further ground combat".
- A loss is of the fire before it: in its combat, else in the step of the shot (Space cannon; Invasion for a bombardment or the space cannon against the landing). The structures of a taken planet are lost in its ground combat. A planet taken is the last line of its ground combat ("Took Meer from Hacan"), or of Invasion when there was no fight ("Took Abyz").
- The summary of a tactical action says the outcome of its combats from its seat: "won the space combat", "lost the space combat", "retreated from the space combat", "space combat: both sides destroyed", "took Meer from Hacan", "lost the ground combat on Meer"; a planet taken is not also "landed on". Ships lost with no combat are "lost 1 ship". Without the fact of the end (a combat under way) it is "combat (lost 1, destroyed 2)".
- An exploration is a line of Exploration in its turn (Primary or Secondaries in a strategic action): "Explored Meer: Volatile Fuel Source", the card by the catalogue.
- The agenda phase has a group for each agenda: "Mutiny · For (For 7 · Against 3)", its outcome and the votes for each outcome (the most first), as the host counted them. Its lines are in the order of the game: the cards played for it ("Played Leadership Rider"), the vote of each seat ("Voted For · 5 votes: Abyz, Fria", the votes of the planets that it exhausted, or "Abstained"), "Broke the tie: For", and how it ended: "Decided by the speaker" (when the speaker broke no tie), "Enacted as a law", "Not enacted", or "Discarded with no effect". A vote after the host resolved an agenda, or a second vote of a seat, is of the next agenda; a card played before the first vote is of the agenda that comes. The summary of the phase is the outcome of each agenda: "Mutiny: For · Shared Research: Against".
- The action card of a component action is named by the card that a decision or the state told ("Component · Sabotage").
- The Log shows what the facts say and adds nothing to it.
