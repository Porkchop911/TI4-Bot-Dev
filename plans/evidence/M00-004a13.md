| Source line | 46 |
| Construction role | Selects legal rollout arms and reports their unconditional inclusion probability. Chooses one mutually-exclusive mode: (1) `uniform_control` — uniform sample across all legal rows; (2) `priority` — required rows + uniform sample within consequential kinds; (3) `exploration` — required rows + uniform sample across all other rows. Unconditional propensity per row is sum of conditional inclusion probability across three modes. Guarantees every legal row non-zero support. Returns `tuple[ArmSelection, ...]` with mode, role, and probability per selected arm. Validates: non-empty choice, positive max_arms, non-negative probabilities summing to 1, incumbent/model options are legal, max_arms >= required-arm count, inclusion probability in (0, 1]. |
