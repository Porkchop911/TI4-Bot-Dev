## Implementation

- `choose_seeing` now scores technology options using the corpus `types` field and the choosing
  seat's face-up technologies. Unit upgrades receive a progress component; coloured cards receive
  a diminishing missing-colour-path component. Unknown or out-of-scope cards retain the blind
  fallback rather than being guessed at.
- Strategy-card choices resolve the offered card through the source-scoped content corpus and use
  the oracle's baseline printed-role values. Printed initiative remains a tiebreak when supplied
  by the window.
- Command-pool allocation now reads visible pool counts in the observed path and applies the
  oracle's `6 / 5 / 3` diminishing-return weights for tactic, strategy, and fleet pools.
