## What was done

Four focused tests added to `crates/ti4-engine/src/game.rs` (test module only, +577 lines, zero
removed lines), each pinning one interaction between an in-flight faction/TE effect and an M06
event-scoped scoring pause:

1. **`munitions_reserves_survive_the_barrage_scoring_pause`** — Letnev `a` (TG=4) vs b
   (fighter×1 + cruiser×8), preloaded faces `[10;4] + [1;600]`. Round 1: a pays Munitions Reserves
   (2 TG, marker `munitions_round = Some(1)`); the fwp barrage window opens and pauses the combat.
   Assertions at the pause: aftermath retained, event-scoring window open, marker intact, TG=2.
   After declining the window: exactly one `"munitions:"` reroll in dice history (round 1 only),
   round-2 offer appears and is declined rather than inherited, combat runs to the MAX_ROUNDS draw
   cleanly. The seeded munitions reroll outcome is deliberately unconstrained — b keeps ≥6 cruisers
   through any reroll, so the test holds whatever the reroll does.

2. **`the_home_loss_pause_holds_the_invasion_at_finalizing_control`** (renamed per review M1b) —
   L1Z1X `a` invades b's home system (pds×2 + spacedock on the planet) with infantry×3, driven
   through the public `InvasionWindow` API in the exact order a Game-level driver sees it: commit →
   ground combat → **ground-combat win occurrence** (`Feat::WonInARivalHome`, combat=true) queued
   first; after that window "closes" (one `settle()`), control is established and the **home-loss
   occurrence** (`Feat::LostAHomePlanet`, combat=false) queues, pausing at FinalizingControl.
   Load-bearing assertions: occurrence order, no conversion before the window closes, control already
   transferred to a at the pause (establish_control precedes it), `captured == [(planet, Some(b))]`
   exactly once, window done after the second settle. **The three unit-conversion assertions are
   vacuous under current behavior** (review M1): because of F-M07-019-1, b's structures die in the
   ground combat, so `standing` at the pause is a's own infantry and every conversion assertion
   passes trivially. They become load-bearing when F-M07-019-1 is fixed (the planet then falls
   without resistance; `standing` holds b's three structures at the pause and must convert
   one-for-one after the resume). Assimilate's conversion itself is covered by the direct
   `control_gained` tests in faction_abilities.rs.

3. **`flank_speed_expires_at_the_activation_boundary_across_a_scoring_pause`** — a holds Flank Speed
   + fwp; b fighter×1 only; dice `[10,10,10,10,1,1]`. The reaction slot
   `reaction:generic:SYSTEM_ACTIVATED:after` auto-plays the single card (activation_seq=1), the fwp
   window pauses combat. Assertions: `move_bonus(seq)==1` at the pause and after completion;
   `move_bonus(seq+1)==0` — expiry is by sequence identity, not by the pause.

4. **`te_breakthrough_survives_the_combat_scoring_pause`** — letnevbt `a` (FULL sources) with a TE
   expedition slice claimed and destroyer×2 at ids[0] + destroyer×1 at ids[1]; b fighter×1 +
   cruiser×4; dice `[10;6, 1,1,1, 7,7,7,7]`. The fwp window pauses mid-combat. Assertions:
   breakthrough and slice intact at the pause and after completion; the Gravleash anchor
   `gravleash_move_values[ids[1]] == Some(2)` persists across the pause and action end (anchors are
   cleared only at the next activation, by design).

## Findings

### F-M07-019-1 — structures count as ground defenders (rules deviation; pre-dates M06)

`invasion.rs::defender_on` counts **any** unit owned by a non-invader on a planet as a defender.
Official LRR: "If a player lands units on a planet controlled by an opponent but that does not
contain any of the opponent's ground forces, that planet falls without resistance." Consequences in
the engine today:

- PDS/Space Dock trigger a full ground combat even though they roll no dice and are not ground
  forces; they can only absorb hits.
- In the full public-API invasion flow, rival structures are destroyed before control transfers, so
  L1Z1X Assimilate's structure conversion is unreachable through `InvasionWindow` (test 2 above pins
  the current behavior: structures die in the fight or convert one-for-one only if they survived).

Pre-dates M06 (M05-era code); `invasion.rs` is not writable in this package. **Escalated to the Tier B
reviewer** — it changes legality/timing semantics of invasions, so per AGENTS.md a frontier-model
adjudication is required before any fix package is scoped.

**Required deliverable of the fix package (per review M1c):** Assimilate-after-pause coverage. Once
structures stop triggering ground combat, `the_home_loss_pause_holds_the_invasion_at_finalizing_control`
must be strengthened so its conversion assertions are load-bearing — in particular the ownership
assertion must check for the l1z1x_ variants (it currently only checks ownership), and the one-for-
one count preservation must hold against b's surviving structures.

### F-M07-019-2 — phantom round rolls after an fwp pause that followed a total barrage wipe (minor)

Reachable when the round-1 Anti-Fighter Barrage both records `BarrageTookTheLastFighters` and
destroys one side's entire fleet. The scoring pause sets stage `RollingAfterBarrage`; on resume,
`roll_round(run_barrage=false)` rolls both fleets **before** any `over()` check (the pre-pause path
checks `over()` immediately after the barrage — 78.3a). Impact analysis:

- Hits against the empty fleet are discarded by the existing 15.2a branch (`alive == 0`), so no
  spurious casualty/sustain choices appear and the final unit state, winner, and events are identical
  to a rules-correct immediate conclusion.
- The only observable difference is dice history / RNG stream position: the surviving fleet's phantom
  rolls consume preload entries (test builds) or advance the production RNG differently than a
  rules-correct engine would. Determinism within this engine is unaffected.
- Reviewer-confirmed refinement of why nothing else diverges: `combat_round_seq`, the
  `COMBAT_ROUND_STARTED` emission, and the faction round-opening offers (Munitions Reserves among
  them) are all inside the `if run_barrage` block at combat.rs:1020–1043, so the phantom round cannot
  advance the round identity, emit an event, or open an ability offer. Only the dice stream moves.

`combat.rs` is not writable in this package. Recorded for reviewer triage; if accepted as behavior,
it should be noted in the known-difference ledger because it makes post-fwp-pause dice sequences
non-comparable with any external reference.

### F-M07-019-3 — `Player.event_feats` is invisible to state equality (review M2; scoped child)

The spec's trap #4 says any new state field written by a faction/TE path must be part of the
projection or the direct-vs-stepped equivalence test fails. `Player.event_feats` (`state.rs:399`) —
the M06 field that gates `did_at_occurrence` and therefore secret-scoring eligibility — is **not
compared** in `Player::PartialEq`, while the rest of the occurrence model is compared at the
`GameState` level (`scored_feat_occurrences`, `feat_occurrence_seq`). The `impl PartialEq for Player`
doc comment enumerates its deliberate exclusions (relic_fragments, leaders,
assimilated_technologies) and `event_feats` is not among them; it also lacks the `// Not compared.`
marker. Probe-confirmed by the reviewer: two states differing only in `event_feats` compare equal.

Practical risk is bounded (a feat-evidence divergence would usually also diverge the scored
objectives, which are compared — detection latency, not permanent blindness), but the package's
headline equivalence invariant is satisfied by omission on precisely the field M06 introduced. The
fix is a one-line change in `ti4-model`, outside this package's writable paths → **scoped as child
package M07-021** (prep spec at `plans/M07-021_EVENT_FEATS_PROJECTION.md`): either add the comparison
(recommended — consistent with the rest of the occurrence model) or add the `// Not compared.`
marker with a recorded reason.

### M4 note — redaction coverage is by argument, not by suite (informational)

The redaction invariant above is answered by construction for this zero-source-change package. The
reviewer records what stays open: `choice.rs::redacted_for` redacts only `action_cards` and
`secret_objectives`, so `Player.event_feats` and `GameState.scored_feat_occurrences` are visible in
full to every viewer. There is **no leak today** — a feat performed and the fact of a score are both
public at a real table, and scoring eligibility derives from `secret_objectives`, which *is*
redacted — but nothing pins that judgement; a later `Feat` variant covering private information would
leak silently. Also noted for the spec: there is no suite named "redaction" (the coverage lives in
the `choice.rs::redacted_for` tests and `ti4-model/src/view.rs`, riding in the workspace run); the
spec's checklist has been corrected to name it accurately.
