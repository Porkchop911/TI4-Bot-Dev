## Tests to add

1. A ground-force cargo option scores as transport towards an active prize and records its named
   components through `choose_seeing`.
2. Landing on a still-uncontrolled planet beats declining.
3. An extra landing on a planet already held by a superior friendly garrison loses to declining.

## Implementation

- `ScoredBot::choose_seeing` now recognizes `load` and `land` alongside the existing tactical
  cases, while plain `choose` retains the blind dispatcher.
- Cargo options derive the unit type from the window's public label (the id is only an index),
  score ground forces as transport and fighters as screens, and include the active system's public
  value.
- Landing validates that the option still names an offered ground force, then rewards taking a
  planet and suppresses a surplus landing where friendly ground forces already outnumber defenders.

## Compatibility evidence and known differences

  capacity, invasion, or combat transition changed.
