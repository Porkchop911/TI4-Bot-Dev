## Research and upgrades (technology/faction tech tests)

### Payment
- Resources from planets/trade goods
- Technology cost = technology record cost field
- Payment validated before research

### Gain
- Technology added to player.technologies
- Colour count updated
- Unit upgrades applied to existing units

### Unit upgrade
- Existing units of base type converted to upgraded variant
- Production builds upgraded variant
- Upgrade card covers unit on faction sheet

### Exhaust/ready
- Technology cards exhausted on research
- Ready at status phase start
- Exhausted technologies cleared at status phase

### Events
| Event | Trigger |
|---|---|
| TECHNOLOGY_RESEARCHED | Technology researched |
| UNIT_UPGRADED | Unit upgraded |
| TECHNOLOGY_GAINED | Technology gained (not researched) |
