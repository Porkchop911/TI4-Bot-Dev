### Conservation properties
- `land`: units removed from space, added to planet (no creation/destruction)
- `remove_from_planet`: units removed from planet, returned to caller
- `add`/`remove`: unit count changes tracked by caller
- `replace_unit`/`replace_planet_unit`: unit identity preserved, stats may change
