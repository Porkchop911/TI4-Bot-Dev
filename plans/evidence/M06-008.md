## Action-card lifecycle (from `engine/action_cards.py`, `engine/reactions.py`)

### Draw
- Draw from action_card_deck during status phase
- +1 card for Neural Motivator technology
- Hand limit enforced (3 cards)

### Hand limit
- Maximum 3 action cards in hand
- Excess cards discarded at draw time
- Discard chosen by player (or random if forced)

### Play
- Action card played during action phase
- Effect resolved immediately
- Card moved to discard pile

### Cancel
- Action card can be cancelled by opponent
- Cancelled card moved to discard
- Cancel effect applied

### Discard
- Discard pile tracks played cards
- Discard can be reshuffled into deck
- Discard count tracked

### Timing
- Cards drawn in initiative order (status phase)
- Cards played during action phase
- Cards discarded at hand limit or end of turn

### Implemented effects
- Action card effects registered in reaction system
- Each effect has timing (WHEN/AFTER)
- Effects resolved by timing resolver
