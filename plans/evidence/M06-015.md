## Leaders lifecycle (from `engine/leaders.py`)

### Lock/unlock
- Leaders locked until conditions met
- Unlock conditions checked each round
- Locked leaders cannot be used

### Ready/exhaust
- Leaders ready at status phase
- Leaders exhausted when used
- Exhausted leaders cannot be used

### Purge
- Leaders purged when conditions met
- Purge effect applied immediately
- Purged leaders removed from game

### Generic timing hooks
- Leader effects registered in timing system
- Effects triggered by events (WHEN/AFTER)
- Effects resolved by timing resolver
