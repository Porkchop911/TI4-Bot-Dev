## Decisions made and rationale

- **`place_structure` is tagged `Content("place_structure")`, not one card.** It is called from at
  least three sites across Construction and a law-modified Warfare/Politics context and does not
  itself know which; attributing it to one card would misrepresent the others.
- **`te6warfare`/`te4construction` use `Content(alias)`, not `StrategyCard`.** The module's own
  header comment states they "share printed names with materially different cards" and are
  dispatched by content id specifically to keep that distinction; using the ordinary card name
  would collapse exactly the distinction the file already goes out of its way to preserve.
- **`ready_planets`'s citation (Rule 34.2) is a judgement call, recorded as one.** It is shared by
  at least Diplomacy and Politics' printed text rather than being its own numbered core rule; 34.2
  (planet readying) is the closest LRR citation and is named here rather than left unstated.
- **No features.rs change**, for the same reason as `OBS-003d`: this package is the typed-context
  foundation, and reading it into policy features is `OBS-008d`'s job.
