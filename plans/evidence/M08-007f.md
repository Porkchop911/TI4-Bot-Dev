## Compatibility evidence and known differences

Only the selecting seat's public face-up trade-good count, public revealed/scored objective
records, and the offered legal option are read. The implementation does not inspect secrets,
decks, a hidden plan, opponent holdings, or objective execution state. It deliberately covers
payment options only: preserving goods in negotiated offers and full oracle scheduling remain
separate future work.
