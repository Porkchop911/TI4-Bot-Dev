# M08-007e — Public purchase-objective reservation

## Package specification

- **Milestone / dependencies:** M08-004; M08-007a-d.
- **Objective:** Let a scored bot preserve public resources or influence for a revealed, unscored
  public purchase objective when making an engine-generated payment choice.
- **Oracle:** `D:\Projects\ti4-engine\engine\bots.py` `PUBLIC_GOALS`,
  `_reserve_for_objective`, and `_objective_spending_pressure` at pinned commit
  `37061c511a4780d4c0719e0342533a498cd4b457`; `engine/production.py` payment choices.
- **Rust edit scope:** `crates/ti4-engine/src/production.rs`, `crates/ti4-policy/src/bot.rs`, this
  evidence file, and `plans/EXECUTION_STATE.md` at completion.
- **Permission / external state:** P0 oracle guard/read-only inspection; P1 local code, tests,
  evidence, branch, and focused commit. No network, ports, generated artifacts, destructive work,
  or external-state changes.
- **Inputs / outputs:** An engine payment option marked with its resource/influence kind and a
  public `Observed` aggregate in; a named reservation component on that already-legal option out.
- **Compatibility class / invariants:** Semantic policy compatibility. The engine keeps payment
  legality and execution unchanged. Payment metadata is additive and stable-ID preserving. The
  policy consults only revealed public aliases, public scored records, public available-spend
  aggregate, and its offered option. It selects the smallest still-plausible single-kind public
  purchase target, rather than inspecting secret goals or making a schedule.
- **Non-goals:** Secret objectives; trade-good, token, fragment, action-card, and combined-cost
  objectives; payment legality/execution; plan persistence/schedules; or exact oracle path ranking.

## Implementation and verification

All production payment options now carry additive `payment_kind` metadata (`resources` or
`influence`) while retaining their existing option IDs, labels, `worth`, and `owed` facts. The
observed scorer uses that metadata only when a public single-kind purchase objective is revealed,
unscored, and at least half funded. It protects the smallest such threshold, a deliberately
bounded approximation of the oracle's active-path planner.

The policy adds a named `objective_reserve` penalty proportional to the remaining reserve
shortfall. Existing payment legality and execution still own which options exist and what happens
after one is selected.

### Mutation check

Replacing the `objective_reserve` penalty with `0.0` made
`public_purchase_objective_preserves_the_smallest_resource_payment` select `large` rather than
`small`. The `-2.0`-per-point shortfall penalty was restored before final verification.

## Compatibility evidence and known differences

- The policy reads only `Observed::revealed_objectives`, `Observed::scored_by`, the public
  available-spend aggregate, and the option supplied by the engine. Secret objectives, decks,
  private holdings, and per-planet exhaustion identities remain inaccessible.
- Payment option identity, availability, validation, and execution are unchanged. The new
  payload field is additive and the engine test verifies both resource and influence values.
- This is intentionally not the oracle's full `BotRoundPlan`: it has no stored active path,
  schedule, trade-good-only/token/fragment/action-card protection, or mixed resource/influence
  planning. Those need dedicated M08-007 children and must not be inferred from this heuristic.
