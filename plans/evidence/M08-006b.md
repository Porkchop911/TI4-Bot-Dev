# M08-006b — Spend, payment, and public trade-term scoring

## Package specification

- **Milestone / dependencies:** M08; M08-004 and M08-006a (`d95227e`).
- **Objective:** Score engine-generated affordable token spends, payment choices, and trade offers
  whose complete terms are encoded in their public stable option IDs.
- **Oracle:** `D:\Projects\ti4-engine\engine\bots.py` at
  `37061c511a4780d4c0719e0342533a498cd4b457`: `_score_strategy_offer`, `_score_payment`, and
  the trade-value helpers.
- **Rust edit scope:** `crates/ti4-policy/src/bot.rs`, this evidence file, and
  `plans/EXECUTION_STATE.md` at completion.
- **Permission / external state:** P0 oracle guard/read; P1 local edits, verification, branch,
  and focused commit. No network, external state, or destructive work.
- **Inputs / outputs:** Legal engine-generated `spend`, `pay`, and `offer` choices in; named
  factual components and a legal offered option out.
- **Compatibility class / invariants:** Semantic policy compatibility. The transaction engine
  remains the sole source of offer construction and atomic legality. Trade acceptance/counter
  choices carry no terms and stay unscored instead of reading private state or parsing a prompt.
- **Non-goals:** Negotiation/counteroffer policy, promissory-note valuation, objective reservations,
  faction personalities, and strategy secondary choices without their effect terms.

## Tests to add

1. A trade good loses to an exact offered payment planet.
2. An affordable three-influence command-token purchase beats decline.
3. Commodity conversion and mutual support beat a one-sided gift.

## Implementation

- `pay` retains its exact-fit rule and now gives a named preservation penalty to the flexible
  `trade_good` option.
- `spend` scores the already-engine-affordable three-influence Leadership purchase as one command
  token minus its printed influence cost. Pool assignment remains the existing token-pool choice.
- `offer` parses only the stable formats produced by `transactions::offer_options`: mutual Support,
  commodity conversion, commodity/trade-good exchange, generic trade-good exchange, and gifts.
  Note sales remain neutral because the current engine's flat price deliberately does not encode
  a note's real strategic value.

## Compatibility evidence and known differences

- `transactions::offer_options` remains the only producer of terms, and its atomic resolution is
  unchanged. The scorer never constructs, rewrites, or accepts a deal.
- Generic `transaction` acceptance/counter and `open_transaction` choices intentionally remain in
  the unscored ledger: their options omit the actual terms, and inferring them from an opaque
  prompt would be neither oracle-compatible nor information-safe.
- The existing flat promissory-note price is a known engine simplification. Assigning a generic
  score to every note would entrench it as policy truth, so note sales are named neutral until
  per-note semantics are ported.
- Objective reserve values, faction personality, and strategy-secondary effect terms remain
  M08-007-or-later work.
