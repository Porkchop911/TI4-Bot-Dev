## Result

- `Choice` has an optional serde-compatible typed context; `DecisionLog` records it.
- `ChoiceOption` has an in-memory analytic preview skipped by serde and excluded from option
  identity.
- Every shared payment face previews the exact requested spendable pool and trade-good count after
  that face. Planet removal uses the card's best available requested-kind face while debt uses the
  selected face's `worth`; the distinction is pinned by preview/application agreement.
- Generic and production-window payment questions carry full bill, paid amount (including
  transaction-local credit), remaining debt, phase and round. Window payments also name the system.
- The explicit policy surface emits debt amount/paid/remaining, payment-option count, stable
  subtype, preview outcome state, exact before/after/change quantities, and the pre-existing
  cover/overpay/shortfall facts under the already-approved `pay` family.
- Unknown and unavailable previews have distinct markers and emit no numeric consequence.
- No payment option, id, label, payload, legality, or state-application path changed.

## Counterfactual and compatibility evidence

- Otherwise-identical payment choices independently vary already-paid debt, option count, and the
  exact preview after/change. Each changes its named fact.
- The paid-debt counterfactual survives `mlp_option_features`, and `projection::admits` confirms the
  `pay` family reaches the dense input.
- Unknown and unavailable previews emit their distinct state markers but no fabricated resource
  before/after/change.
- A previewed option serializes without the preview, reads back with none, and remains equal.
- A contextualized decision records its context. Its V1 hash equals the context-stripped record's
  V1 hash, preserving the `OBS-003b` backward contract.
- Every offered planet and trade-good face was applied to a cloned test fixture only for the
  agreement assertion; the preview implementation itself is analytic and never clones/applies.
