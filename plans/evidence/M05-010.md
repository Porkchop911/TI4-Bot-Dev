### Extra dice
- Graviton: +1 die per gun (one per owner)
- Plasma: +1 die per gun (once per owner, per combat)
- Both stack (additive)

### Rerolls
- `offer_unit_ability_rerolls(game, owner, roll, system_id, source)`
- Unit abilities that reroll dice
- Reroll offered to owner, not opponent

### Morale
- Applied when announcing retreats
- Reduces dice for retreating player
- Scoped to combat round

### Munitions
- Applied to cruiser dice
- Extra dice for cruiser combat value
- Scoped to combat

### No effect leakage
- Modifiers scoped to specific combat
- Not carried across combats
- Not carried across activations
- Technology effects checked per-activation
