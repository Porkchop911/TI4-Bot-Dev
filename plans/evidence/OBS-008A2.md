## Result

1. **Engine.** `tactical::preview_moves(state, content, sources, player, active, choice)` builds the
   unit catalogue once, takes `fleet::standing_using` for the active system with no arrival, and for
   every option carrying a `unit` payload takes it again with a single
   `Arrival { count: 1, in_space: true }` of that unit type. Each ship-move option
   (`move|…` / `move_gd|…`) gets `Preview::certain` with two deltas: `FleetSupplyHeadroom` and
   `CapacityFree`, before → after. The "finish movement" decline option keeps no preview. It shares
   `fleet::standing_using` with production placement (OBS-008c2b) and end-of-turn enforcement, so
   the arrival preview and the removal that may follow cannot drift apart. `game.rs` calls it once
   the movement choice is built, only when an active system exists.
2. **Policy.** `tactical_decision_features` (OBS-008a1) already guards on
   `subtype ∈ {activate_system, movement_step}` and walks `option.preview`; its delta match now
   also maps `FleetSupplyHeadroom → fleet-headroom` and `CapacityFree → capacity-free`, emitting
   `tactical:{quantity}-{before,after,change}` under `tactical:preview-known`. No new family.
3. **Boundaries.** Previews are `#[serde(skip)]`: no option ID, label, payload, legal set, replay
   script, or V1/V2 decision hash moves. No `DecisionContext` field is added or changed —
   `movement_step` still carries no `optional` flag and no `target`; those are a later, separately
   reviewed context touch. Destination-only: the origin losing a hull is not previewed, and a move
   spends no resource/influence/trade-good/token pool.

## Focused evidence

- `tactical.rs::obs008a2_move_options_preview_the_arriving_ships_fleet_and_capacity_effect`: a
  carrier and a cruiser at an outer hub tile, both moving into the activated centre. The carrier
  option previews `CapacityFree` change `+carrier.capacity` and `FleetSupplyHeadroom` change `-1`;
  the cruiser option previews `CapacityFree` change `0` and `FleetSupplyHeadroom` change `-1`; the
  decline option has no preview. The carrier preview's `after` capacity is then checked against
  `fleet::standing` recomputed once a carrier is actually placed in the centre — two independent
  computations.
- `features.rs::obs008a2_move_options_carry_the_arriving_ships_fleet_and_capacity_effect`: a
  `movement_step` choice with a previewed carrier move and a bare `done_moving` option.
  `tactical:fleet-headroom-{before,after,change}` = 3 / 2 / -1 and
  `tactical:capacity-free-{after,change}` = 4 / 4 reach the policy (a genuine-zero `before` is a
  dropped sparse entry); the decline option carries `tactical:subtype:movement_step` and
  `tactical:option-count` but no consequence fact; the facts survive `mlp_option_features` and
  `projection::admits`.
