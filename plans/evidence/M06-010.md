## Secret objectives (from `engine/secrets.py`)

### Timing
| Phase | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Status | Drawn during setup (1 card per player) |
| Action | Scored when conditions met |
| Agenda | May be scored during agenda |
| Combat | May be scored during combat |

### Limits
- Maximum 3 secret objectives in hand
- Excess discarded at draw time
- Discarded when scored or purged

### Score/purge behavior
- Scored when conditions met → VP awarded
- Purged when conditions not met → card removed
- Purged at end of round if unplayed
- Purged at end of game if unplayed

### Secret objective effects
- Each secret has unique score condition
- Condition checked each round
- Condition checked after each relevant event
