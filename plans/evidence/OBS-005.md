## Result

`Observed` gains `OpponentRelationship` (`CombatCounterpart` > `Support` > `Neighbor` > `None`,
ranked by declared strategic salience since the contract lists the three kinds without fixing a
tie-break order) and `opponent_slots(player)`, which sorts every other seat by
`(relationship, initiative_rank, seating_offset)` ascending. Because the engine always seats exactly
six players (`seating.rs`), there are always exactly five opponent slots — a closed, bounded family
with no player-count generalisation problem.

`crates/ti4-policy/src/features.rs::opponent_slot_facts` emits up to seven bounded facts per slot
under one new family, `opponent-slot`, reading only `PublicSeat`/`OpponentRelationship` — no card
identity, no player/planet/system name.

## Focused evidence

- `crates/ti4-engine/src/choice.rs::obs005_opponent_slots_are_relationship_then_initiative_then_seating`:
  a six-seat position with one combat counterpart, one Support tie, one galaxy-adjacent neighbor,
  and two unrelated seats separated only by initiative, asserts the exact slot order.
- `crates/ti4-engine/src/choice.rs::obs005_opponent_slots_are_invariant_under_player_id_relabeling`:
  the contract's required permutation-equivariance property. The same relative structure, built
  from two disjoint sets of `PlayerId`s, produces an identical sequence of
  `OpponentRelationship` classifications across slots.
- `crates/ti4-policy/src/features.rs::obs005_opponent_slot_facts_are_relationship_relative_not_identity_relative`:
  putting the identical combat relationship on a different concrete seat emits identical facts
  (slot 0's `relationship-combat`/`victory-points`), and removing the relationship changes what is
  emitted — proving the family is relationship-relative, not player-ID-relative, and not inert.
- `EXPLICIT_FIXED_FAMILIES`/`live_grammar_families`, `FAMILY_ROLES` classification coverage, and the
  legacy schema-4 baseline pin all updated and pass with `opponent-slot` registered and excluded
  from the frozen legacy pin.

## Vocabulary migration (v5 → v6)

`OOV_REGISTRY_VERSION` 5 → 6. `OOV_FAMILIES_V6` appends `"opponent-slot"` to `OOV_FAMILIES_V5`
(append-only). New `OOV_FAMILIES_V6_FINGERPRINT` (computed by `registry_fingerprint`). The
one-version-back inference window shifts: v6 loads for training and inference, v5 loads for
inference only, v4 is now refused exactly as v2/v3 are. Renamed/renumbered the affected tests:
`version_five_loads_for_inference_without_renumbering_its_columns` (was the v4 test) and
`version_four_remains_refused_for_inference` (new, pins the shifted refusal boundary); the prior
`version_three_remains_refused_for_inference` is kept as a plain historical regression (still true,
no longer the boundary case).
