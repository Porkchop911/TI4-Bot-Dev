## What was done

Additive module `crates/ti4-sim/src/behavior.rs` (registered in `lib.rs`; no other file under
`crates/` changed — `run.rs`, `play()`, and `GameResult` untouched, per the spec's non-goals).
The suite:

1. **Fixed seed set** — 30 seeds `812_001..=812_030` (committed constant; consecutive integers in
   an unused range, no other property claimed).
2. **Protocol v1** — seats `p1`..`p6` on the stable roster, `DEFAULT` (= FULL) content scope —
   the simulator runtime's assigned scope; an earlier draft of this record mislabeled it POK,
   corrected 2026-08-23 under M08-019 E2. All v1/v2/v3 bounds were generated under FULL, so no
   measurement is relabeled in either direction — only the protocol claim was wrong.
   `Seats::Scored` (one authored bot per seat), `Horizon::default()`. Games end by objective
   exhaustion in about nine rounds; all thirty v1 games ended cleanly (27 `ObjectivesExhausted`,
   3 `VictoryPoints`).
3. **Ten metrics** — `vp_pace` (mean final VPs / rounds, averaged over seeds), `completion`
   (clean endings), `score_spread` (per-game population std dev of the six final scores —
   within-game dispersion, invariant to which seat scored what), `faction_differentiation`
   (population std dev of the six per-faction mean VPs across the seed set — the spec's
   "spread across the seated factions"; added in the review-resolution pass, finding V3), and an
   action mix: each tactical event label's share of a game's event stream, averaged over seeds.
   Labels: `SYSTEM_ACTIVATED`, `SHIP_MOVED`, `PRODUCTION_RESOLVED`, `INVASION_RESOLVED`,
   `SPACE_COMBAT_RESOLVED`, `TACTICAL_ACTION_BEGAN` (chosen from the v1 census — see below).
4. **Bounds** — 95% bootstrap CIs (2000 resamples per metric, deterministic splitmix64) over the
   baseline's per-seed values, embedded as constants in `baseline_bounds()`. Re-baseline
   discipline is stated in the module docs: old/new side by side here, semantic cause named,
   review approval; an out-of-bounds result on an unchanged tree is a failure to diagnose.
5. **Gate test** — plays the seed set twice, asserts per-seed identity (VPs, rounds, decisions,
   events, ending) *before* any comparison (a flaky bound could otherwise hide engine
   nondeterminism), pins the metric/bounds key sets to be exactly equal (review V4), checks all
   ten metrics against the recorded bounds, and recomputes every bound from current data under
   the named protocol constants (`BOOTSTRAP_DRAWS`, `BOOTSTRAP_SEED`) asserting bit-equality.

## v9 — 2026-08-30: Sabotage cancels the card being played

Cause: the engine-completion handoff's Cancel API group. Sabotage (all four copies,
`sabo1`–`sabo4`) moved from an unimplemented alias to a working cancellation: "When another
player plays an action card other than 'Sabotage': cancel that action card."

The machinery was already in place — `announce()` emits `ACTION_CARD_PLAYED` through the
resolver and skips the card's effect when the event comes back cancelled, and the Sabotage
window row already existed in the window table. This group added the two missing pieces:

- **The cancellation itself.** The reaction slot that owns the triggering `ACTION_CARD_PLAYED`
  event is the only code that still holds that event (a card effect's signature carries no
  event, and by the time the played card's effect runs the triggering event is back in its
  own frame). The slot cancels the event after a successfully played Sabotage; the card is
  still spent (1.15 cancels the event, not the spend) and its effect never runs. The effect
  table entry exists so a played Sabotage reports as resolved rather than
  `ACTION_CARD_UNRESOLVED`.
- **The "other than 'Sabotage'" guard.** The window row's guard now reads the played card's
  alias off the event payload, so the four copies cancel other cards being played, not each
  other — a chain of Sabotages would spend the whole deck for nothing.

Like the card groups before it this is a change in **play**: the bots now hold and play
Sabotage, so a card play the v8 bots let stand can now be interrupted mid-announcement. The
shifts are modest: `faction_differentiation` and `score_spread` widen (upper bounds rise — a
play that can be undone is another way for endings to split), the action-label shares drift by

## v11 — 2026-08-30: the turn-flow cards become real (Deadly Plot, Coup d'Etat, Crisis, Master Plan)

Cause: the engine-completion handoff's turn group. Four action cards moved from
unimplemented aliases to working effects, and the turn machinery gained three typed events
(`STRATEGIC_ACTION_BEGAN`, `TURN_PASSED`, `ACTION_COMPLETED`) so reaction windows can bind to
the moments the cards watch:

- **Deadly Plot** (`deadly_plot`): "When an agenda is resolved: if you did not vote or
  predict its outcome, you may discard the agenda instead of resolving it. Then, exhaust all
  of your planets." The vote/prediction mirror lives on `GameState` for the duration of the
  `AGENDA_RESOLVED` window only; the guard compares the holder's cast vote and standing
  prediction against the resolved outcome. A discarded agenda runs no effect, pays nothing,
  elects no feat, and (this engine never draws a replacement after a resolution) spends the
  card. The card's tail exhausts every planet the holder controls.
- **Coup d'Etat** (`coup`): "When another player begins a strategic action, you may end
  their turn instead." The engine sets a transient flag in the card's effect and checks it in
  the strategic branch of the action driver immediately after `STRATEGIC_ACTION_BEGAN`: the
  active player's turn ends before the strategic action is resolved, and the strategy card
  stays in hand, unspent and unexhausted.
- **Crisis** (`crisis`): "When you pass: if at least two other players have not yet passed,
  the next player's turn is skipped." The guard counts unpassed seats from `GameState`; the
  skip is consumed by `advance_turn` on the seat the turn just arrived at, emitting
  `TURN_SKIPPED:<player>` with no end-of-turn window.
- **Master Plan** (`master_plan`): "When you play an action card, you may perform an
  additional action on this turn." The grant is consumed at the next turn boundary:
  `advance_turn` retains the turn (emitting `TURN_RETAINED`) instead of running
  end-of-turn processing, so the second action happens in the same turn. An explicit pass
  clears the grant first.

## v14 — 2026-08-31: the salvage/repair/infiltrate/black-market/reverse-engineer cards become real

Cause: the engine-completion handoff's sub-batch C3. Five action cards moved from
unimplemented aliases to working effects, and the driver gained the moments those cards
watch:

- **Salvage** (`salvage`): "After you win a space combat: Your opponent gives you all of
  their commodities." The driver's `SPACE_COMBAT_WON` emission became a typed event
  (After, naming the winner and, in a handoff field, the other sides of the fight, because
  the window that follows cannot read the payload that summoned it) and now propagates
  timing errors through the new `CombatError::Timing` variant instead of swallowing them.
- **Reparations** (`reparations`): "After another player gains control of a planet you
  control: Exhaust 1 planet that player controls and ready 1 planet you control." The
  control-gain settlement records the previous owner before it changes hands; the effect
  asks the new owner which of their planets to exhaust and reads the holder's own planets
  (LRR 42.1).
- **Infiltrate** (`infiltrate`): "When you gain control of a planet: Replace each PDS and
  space dock that is on that planet with a matching unit from your reinforcements." With a
  full box the replacement is the same unit in the unit-less model, so the effect performs
  the faithful remove-and-conditional-re-add and the driver tests pin the play, the spend,
  and an undisturbed capture.
- **Black Market Dealings** (`blackmarketdealing`): "When you are negotiating a
  transaction: You and the other player may include relics, action cards, and unscored
  secret objectives as part of the transaction. This card cannot be canceled." The
  transaction's opening is now a typed `TRANSACTION_OPENED` (When, naming both chairs);
  the play sets the `BLACK_MARKET` flag, which clears on completion and at the turn's
  end, and widens `offer_options` with flat one-good shapes for unscored secret
  objectives (`so{id}:1`) and relic fragments (`fr{trait}:1`) that `take`/`give` move
  like any other shape.
- **Reverse Engineer** (`reverse_engineer`): "After another player discards an action
  card that has a component action: Take that action card from the discard pile." Every
  action-card play now converges on an `ACTION_CARD_DISCARDED` event (After, naming the
  discarder and the card) that is pushed onto the new `discarded_action_cards` pile; the
  effect verifies the discarder is not the holder and the card is a component action,
  then lifts it out of the pile.

Three of the ten point estimates fell outside their v13 intervals
(`share_PRODUCTION_RESOLVED`, `share_SYSTEM_ACTIVATED`, `share_TACTICAL_ACTION_BEGAN`) —
the signature of the new dilution sources: every component play appends an
`ACTION_CARD_DISCARDED` event and the reaction slots the new windows open add steps and
window events to every stream the shares divide by. Simulating seats now also *play* the
new cards from those windows (the default decider takes the first offered option, and a
reaction play is offered before the decline): Reverse Engineer pulls played component
cards out of the pile, which reshuffles the action-card supply, and Black Market Dealings
lets both sides price secrets and cards into trades. The consequence is equalization —
`faction_differentiation` [0.378, 0.841] -> [0.312, 0.746] and `score_spread`
[1.531, 1.903] -> [1.423, 1.791] both fall and narrow: the earlier duress-vintage swings
are softened by commodities changing hands for free (Salvage) and by cards and secrets
circulating through black-market trades. `vp_pace` slips [0.391, 0.443] -> [0.377, 0.438]
as the extra window questions lengthen action phases. `completion` stays the strict 1.0
invariant — `SPACE_COMBAT_WON` now propagates timing errors instead of swallowing them,
and every game in the suite still ends cleanly, so the strict gate holds under the new
error surface.
