## Agenda voting (from `engine/agenda.py`)

### Eligible voters
- All players with ships in systems
- Players with influence (from planets)
- Players with trade goods (can be spent)

### Influence payment
- Each player pays influence to vote
- Payment from controlled planets
- Payment validated before vote

### Abstain
- Player may abstain from voting
- Abstention = no vote cast
- Abstention does not cost influence

### Tie
- Tie broken by speaker (agenda phase)
- Speaker's vote counts twice in tie
- Tie resolved before agenda effect applied

### Speaker
- Speaker determined by strategy phase
- Speaker votes first in agenda
- Speaker has tie-breaking advantage

### Result
- Agenda effect applied after voting
- Effect depends on vote outcome
- Effect may modify game state
