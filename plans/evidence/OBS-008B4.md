## Result

1. **The gap.** OBS-008b1 gave casualty and sustain options their `unit` identity but attached no
   preview to either — sustain was the one option kind in that package left with no consequence
   fact at all.
2. **Engine.** Both option-building sites (`offer_sustain`, and the windowed `Sustaining` stage
   that duplicates it) now attach `Preview::certain` with one `ShipsInSystem` delta, computed once
   per ask (shared by every option in it, not recomputed per option): the sustain option previews
   no change (the ship survives, damaged); the decline ("take the hit") option previews the seat's
   own ship count in the system falling by exactly one (the ship is destroyed).
3. **Policy.** `combat_decision_features`'s subtype guard now also matches `sustain_damage`; the
   existing `ShipsInSystem -> "ships"` mapping (from OBS-008b3) needed no change. No new family,
   no vocabulary bump.
4. **Boundaries.** Previews are `#[serde(skip)]`; no option ID, label, payload, legal set, replay
   script, or V1/V2 decision hash moves. No `DecisionContext` field is added or changed. Sustain
   legality and `sustainers` are untouched.

## Focused evidence

- `combat.rs::obs008b4_sustain_options_preview_the_fleet_surviving_or_falling_by_one`: a
  dreadnought and a cruiser (2 ships total) at one system. The sustain option previews
  `ShipsInSystem` `2 -> 2`; the decline option previews `2 -> 1` — through both the standalone
  `offer_sustain` and the windowed `Sustaining` stage on a fresh fixture (the first call already
  damaged the dreadnought, so the windowed check needs its own undamaged position).
- `features.rs::obs008b4_sustain_options_carry_subtype_and_exact_ship_counts`: a `sustain_damage`
  choice with both options previewed directly. `combat:subtype:sustain_damage`,
  `combat:option-count` (2), and exact `combat:ships-{before,after}` (2/2 for sustain, 2/1 for
  decline) reach the policy; sustaining's genuine-zero `ships-change` is a dropped sparse entry;
  the decline option's arrival fact survives `mlp_option_features` and `projection::admits`.
