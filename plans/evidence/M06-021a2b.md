## Implemented behavior

- Bombardment allocates a separate non-combat occurrence and opens an unlimited action window.
- Each defended planet allocates a distinct ground-combat occurrence with a one-score-per-player cap.
- Losing control of a home-system planet records the former controller at the loss boundary; the
  invasion pauses before gain-control effects, technology effects, exploration, or later captures.
- The last player to pass receives a distinct action occurrence before the action phase advances.
- Every resolved agenda receives a distinct agenda occurrence, including For/Against outcomes;
  player or controlled-planet elections additionally record exact elected-player attribution.
- Failed known secret awards cannot fall through to public scoring. The legacy turn-scoped feat and
  event-window paths have been removed.
- Direct synchronous combat and invasion wrappers consume internal scoring pauses while preserving
  occurrence facts, so they still run their rule sequence to completion.
- Space-cannon scoring closes before combat opens, and one agenda's scoring closes before the next
  agenda is revealed or installed in state.
- Internal emitters require a concrete `FeatOccurrence`; there is no optional silent-drop path.

## Focused evidence

The focused regressions cover barrage timing and cross-window combat caps, space cannon,
separate bombardments, separate ground combats per planet, exact home-loss pause/resume,
