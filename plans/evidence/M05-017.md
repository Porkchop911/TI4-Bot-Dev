### Discounts
| Discount | Effect | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Integrated Economy | -1 cost per unit type | technology |
| Free production | 0 cost for specific units | faction tech |
| Construction | Free structure | strategy card |

### Free-production sequence
1. Construction strategy card → free structure
2. Faction free production → free units
3. Integrated Economy → -1 discount
4. Remaining cost paid from planets/trade goods

### Integrated Economy constraints
- Only applies to one unit type per player
- Cannot reduce cost below 0
- Must be researched before use
