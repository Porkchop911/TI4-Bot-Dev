## Campaign and result

- One focused campaign runs exactly 100 native seeded games (`0..100`) across two through six
  players, cycling the player count per seed. Each game receives a 500-step bound.
- Every run reaches `RunError::Step(GameError::StatusChoicesUnimplemented)` in `Phase::Status`.
  This is the expected, explicit boundary: it proves the structural driver reaches status without
  illegal choices, deadlock, deck-underflow success, or a hidden unbounded loop; it does **not**
  claim that any game finishes.
- The same-seed campaign snapshots serialized state, canonical event sequence, decision log, and
  `StepResult` after every step and requires exact equality across repeat runs.
- Every recorded decision is asserted to belong to its offered option set.
