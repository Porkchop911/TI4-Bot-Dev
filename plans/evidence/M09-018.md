## Part 1 — Hashing: PASS

- Implementation (`learned.rs::bucket`) matches the documented spec and the oracle's `_bucket`
  (pinned `37061c5`, extracted to `out/tmp-ref/learned_policy_pinned.py` for comparison): blake2b
  with an 8-byte digest, first four bytes little-endian modulo dimensions, sign from the low bit of
  byte five. One documented divergence: Rust treats `dimensions == 0` as 1 (degenerate case) where
  the oracle raises `ValueError`.
- **Independent re-derivation:** all 48 rows of `crates/ti4-policy/tests/golden_buckets.json` were
  recomputed with Python `hashlib.blake2b` and compared to the stored golden values:
  **0 mismatches** (cross-library check: Rust `blake2` crate vs CPython hashlib).
- Corpus adequacy: 12 distinct names × dimensions {1, 7, 512, 4096}; names cover empty string,
  single char, `board.planets=6`, `faction=sol`, `head=movement|opt=move|16|0`, `kind=strategy`,
  `option|18`, `player.trade_goods`, `prompt:activate a system`, `round=9`, a non-ASCII unicode
  name, and a 100-char name — i.e. the real feature-string formats of this codebase.

## Part 3 — Softmax stability / inference numerics: PASS

`inference.rs::probabilities`: empty → empty; single option → {id: 1.0}; `temperature.max(1e-6)`;
**shift by the best score before exponentiating** (overflow-safe); non-finite or ≤0 total → uniform
fallback rather than NaN propagation. Sampling: pinned `ChaCha8Rng::seed_from_u64`, inverse-CDF over
the engine's option order with last-option residual default. Pinned by tests: distribution sums to 1
(1e-12), a 1000/999 score pair stays finite and ordered, cold commits harder than warm, empirical
draw rate within 0.08 of the stated probability over 400 draws, same seed → same game, different
seed differs, empty choice refused. Load-time guards (`Profile::validate`): schema ∈ {2,3,4,5}, mode
`fully_learned`, faction match, required heads per schema, all weights finite, temperature finite and
> 0.
