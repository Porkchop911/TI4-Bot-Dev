## Decisions made and rationale

- **`pick` and `predicted_outcome` are card-agnostic, not attributed to one card.** `pick` alone
  has roughly 25 real call sites across the file. Threading a card alias into every one is a much
  larger change than typing 12 producers, and out of this slice's scope; their context instead
  states what kind of thing is being picked (`pick_{kind}`) or that a prediction is being made
  (shared by every rider), which is honest about what the generic helper actually knows.
- **The reparations test fixture required care to actually reach an ask.** An initial attempt gave
  each side only one candidate planet, which the function correctly takes without asking (no
  decision, no log line) — the test asserted "at least one ask reached" and failed, which is the
  fixture being wrong, not the code. Fixed by giving both the gainer and the previous holder two
  candidates each.
- **Split from the rest of `OBS-003h`.** The plan's own row explicitly allows splitting by
  crate/file when it exceeds atomic size; `action_cards.rs` alone (12 producers) is comparable in
  size to `OBS-003d`'s combined four files, and the remaining `OBS-003h` files
  (`faction_abilities.rs`, `reactions.rs`, `relics.rs`, `exploration.rs`, `thunders_edge.rs`,
  `secrets.rs`, `laws.rs`) are recorded as a following slice rather than attempted together.
