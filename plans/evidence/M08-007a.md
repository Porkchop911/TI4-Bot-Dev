# M08-007a — Objective award prioritisation

## Package specification

- **Milestone / dependencies:** M08; M08-004 and completed M08-006a/b.
- **Objective:** Prioritise legal objective-score choices by their printed public point award,
  without reading secrets not offered to the current player.
- **Oracle:** `D:\Projects\ti4-engine\engine\bots.py` at
  `37061c511a4780d4c0719e0342533a498cd4b457`: `goals`, `demands`, and scoring-schedule
  foundations; `engine/objectives.py` for printed points.
- **Rust edit scope:** `crates/ti4-policy/src/bot.rs`, this evidence file, and
  `plans/EXECUTION_STATE.md` at completion.
- **Permission / external state:** P0 oracle guard/read; P1 local code/evidence edits,
  verification, branch, and focused commit. No network, external state, or destructive work.
- **Inputs / outputs:** Engine-generated legal `score` choices in; named point components and an
  offered legal score choice out.
- **Compatibility class / invariants:** Semantic policy compatibility. The scoring window alone
  decides which public/secret objectives are legal and visible. The scorer reads only the option
  itself plus source-scoped public corpus metadata; it never enumerates a hand or an objective deck.
- **Non-goals:** Partial-progress demand planning, spend reservations, future score schedules,
  secret inspection beyond the offered option, and changing score-window legality.

## Tests to add

1. A legal printed two-point public objective beats a legal one-point public objective.

## Implementation

- `score` resolves only its offered alias through the source-scoped public/secret objective corpus
  and multiplies the exact printed point value by the existing victory priority.
- The scorer accepts the older `score|<alias>` test/log shape as well as current scoring-window
  aliases. An unknown alias safely preserves the existing one-point priority.

## Compatibility evidence and known differences

- The objective/scoring window still determines whether an alias is scoreable and whether it is
  public or belongs to the selecting player's secret hand. This scorer receives only that legal
  offered alias.
- There is no scan of the objective deck, another player's secret objectives, or a copied
  unredacted state. Corpus metadata is source-scoped to the current game configuration.
- This child intentionally handles award selection only. Oracle-style partial-progress demands,
  reservations, and round schedules require their own public fact model and remain M08-007b.
