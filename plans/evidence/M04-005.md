## Implementation and invariants

- `strategy_options` uses the existing `next_strategy_picker` snake-order function and offers
  only `unclaimed_strategy_cards` with stable content ids and the oracle's `"N. Name"` label.
- `take_strategy_card` recomputes the current generated choice and calls the central `validate`
  boundary. An invented id cannot be applied.
- All fallible checks complete before mutation. A valid pick deals exactly one card to the current
  picker (using the state's initiative-sorted holding method) and then removes that same card from
  the mat.
- The three-player completion test observes `a, b, c, c, b, a`, gives each player two cards, and
  leaves two cards unclaimed.
