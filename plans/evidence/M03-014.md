reset to one, initiative `sol, letnev`, active player `sol`, AFTER registered before WHEN, and a
non-null no-op event resolver. Exact command:


## Invariants and compatibility contract

- The fixture proves relation partitioning rather than registration order: AFTER is registered
  first, but the WHEN event step appears first.
- The Rust test compares the complete four-line trace and all event fields used by the oracle
  projection, not aggregate effect outcomes.
- M03-011 initially added a depth suffix to public `emit` lines, which would make trace parity
  impossible. The depth remains in private diagnostics and public traces retain the oracle format.
- This single fixture is direct decision-boundary evidence, not a claim that the absent exporter
  offers a comprehensive timing corpus. Additional cases remain covered by the focused ports of
  every applicable `test_timing.py` behavior in M03-009 through M03-012.

- The direct oracle fixture is represented as an inline Rust regression test. It preserves the
  oracle's one-based supplied event ID, cancellation projection, relation order, owner, ability
