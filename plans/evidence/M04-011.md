## Implemented behavior

- `resolve_agenda_phase` rejects non-agenda phases and an agenda phase before custodians removal
  without mutating state.
- It removes up to two top-deck agenda aliases in reveal order, and records a deterministic
  speaker-clockwise voting order for each reveal.
- It explicitly reports each resolution as `Deferred`: no vote, tie-break, law/directive effect,
  timing window, or invented result is applied.
- It readies exhausted planets after both agenda slots, including when the agenda deck is empty.
- The resolver is deliberately not wired through `advance_phase`; M04-012 owns the generated
  decision windows and safe integration for status scoring/token allocation and agenda voting.
