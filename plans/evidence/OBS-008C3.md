## Decisions made and rationale

- **No new feature family.** `discount`/`discount_offered` are read by the existing
  `production_decision_features`, under the already-approved transferable `production` family —
  consistent with `OBS-008c1`/`c2a`/`c2b`'s own choice not to open a new vocabulary namespace before
  every production subtype is measured.
- **The AI Development Algorithm ask reuses the `"produce"` canonical kind** rather than inventing a
  new top-level family for one ask, since its facts (a discount, a technology alias) are exactly the
  shape `production_decision_features` already reads.
- **Harrugh Gefhara's own ask is not exposed**, because it has no invocation path in real play at
  all (`plans/BUG_2026-09-04_LEADER_USE_UNREACHABLE.md`) — there is no live choice to attach a
  feature to yet. Recorded as a boundary, not silently skipped.
