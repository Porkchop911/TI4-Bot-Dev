## Generic strategy secondary (eligibility, payment, decline, completion)

### Secondary eligibility
- Offered after primary effect resolved
- Eligibility depends on card and game state
- Generic: secondary always eligible (structural)

### Strategy token payment
- Each secondary costs 1 strategy token
- Player must have strategy tokens available
- Payment deducted from player's pool

### Decline option
- Player may decline secondary if they wish
- Decline recorded in GameState
- No penalty for declining

### Completion tracking
