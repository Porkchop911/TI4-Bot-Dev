# M04-009 — Generic strategy secondary

## Package specification

- **Milestone / dependencies:** M04; M04-008 complete.
- **Objective:** Provide the structural follower window for a strategic action: clockwise
  eligibility, strategy-token payment, decline, ordered completion tracking, and delayed
  exhaustion of the primary card.
- **Python oracle:** `engine/strategy.py`: `resolve`, `_offer`, and `_spend_strategy_token`;
  `engine/game.py:_take_action`; `tests/test_three_player_strategy.py` strategic-action cases.

- **Permission class:** P1. Writable paths were `crates/ti4-engine/src/strategy.rs`,

  external process, artifact, or external-state effects.

## Implementation and compatibility

- `begin_strategic_action` validates the same generated strategic-action choice as M04-008, then
  opens a `StrategySecondaryWindow` instead of immediately exhausting its selected card.
- Followers are fixed in `GameState::clockwise_from(primary)[1..]`, never initiative order; each
  eligible follower receives stable `decline` and `follow` options.
- `follow` spends exactly one token from the strategy pool. A tokenless follower is deterministically
  recorded `Ineligible` without a choice; a decline costs nothing. Results retain resolution order.
- The selected primary card exhausts only after every follower has resolved or been skipped,
  matching LRR 82.1–82.2's order. Content-specific secondary effects and extra eligibility checks
  remain intentionally out of scope.
- The M04-008 `take_strategic_action` helper remains as its immediate-exhaustion structural-primary
  compatibility API. Future action drivers must use the new window; M04-012 will own open windows
  in the game step state.
