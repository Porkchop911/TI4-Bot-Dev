## F-M09-030-P1-1 — HIGH: schema-6 manifest validation was fail-open

The M09-028 loader verified only part of the manifest contract. It ignored trunk depth and
activation, embedding dimension, student temperature, pinned `tch`/libtorch/compiler/thread
settings, and provenance structure; it accepted a missing or malformed `update` as zero. Its list
helper discarded non-string entries, its checksum map permitted undeclared extras, and the writer
interpolated provenance strings without JSON escaping. `latest_complete` also treated an unreadable
manifest as committed merely because the path was a file.

This is load-bearing rather than documentation drift: those fields determine architecture,
reproducibility, and artifact identity, and MLP Plan §4.4 explicitly says loading verifies them.

### Correction submitted

- the writer uses `serde_json` encoding and records the exact compiler supplied by the build;
- publication rejects empty sources and non-hex/unrecorded commit identities;
- the loader requires the exact schema-6 field inventory and fixed trunk, embedding, temperature,
  runtime, thread, provenance, slot-bound, and checksum contracts;
- `update` is mandatory, list entries are strictly strings, and checksum keys exactly equal the
  files required by the selected critic mode;
- recovery ignores missing, oversized, and syntactically unreadable manifests;
- focused regressions mutate each formerly ignored class and prove refusal, while quoted/newline
  provenance round-trips as valid JSON.

Fresh gates after correction: `cargo test -p ti4-mlp --lib bundle` **12/0** and full
`cargo test -p ti4-mlp` **59 + 3 + 3 + 2 + 3 doctests / 0**.

### Artifact invalidation

The retained bundles under `out/checkpoints/mlp`, `out/checkpoints/mlp-critic`, and
`out/checkpoints/mlp-cuda` predate this correction and omit `compiler`. They are rejected by the
corrected loader. They must be republished through the committed writer (or regenerated by their
own package); editing immutable bundle directories in place is not an acceptable migration.
