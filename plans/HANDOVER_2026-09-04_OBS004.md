## Relevant existing boundary

`crates/ti4-engine/src/choice.rs` already provides a capability-bound `SeatObservation`:

- `held_secrets()`
- `held_action_cards()`
- `held_promissory_notes()` (only facedown notes in the bound seat's hand)
- `held_secret_progress()`

These have **no player argument**. Preserve that rule: policy code must not regain a `GameState`,
`Player`, arbitrary seat ID, deck order, or an accessor that can name another player's holdings.
`Observed` exposes public facts only; faceup promissory notes are public through
`Observed::promissory_notes()`.
