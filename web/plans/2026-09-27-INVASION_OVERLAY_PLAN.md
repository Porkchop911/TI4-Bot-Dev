## Goal

Give the invader, defenders, other players, and spectators one understandable, live view of an invasion, from the `INVASION_BEGAN` reaction through bombardment, landing, each planet's ground combat, and control/exploration hand-off. While choosing how many forces to land, show **planet-specific projected invasion odds** for the currently drafted commitment against the actual defenders; adding a force updates the estimate. Offer cards and follow-up decisions in that view, but submit only engine-offered option IDs. A completed space battle must not hold the invasion behind its automatic recap.

Suggested presentation: `Invasion begins → Bombardment → Plan landings + projected odds → Commit landings → Space cannon defense → Ground battle (before rolls → round results → next round) → Planet result → Next planet / invasion complete`. These are _public presentation states_, not promises that every automatic engine step will pause for input. Show the current planet and the overall invasion progress separately; several planets can be invaded in one tactical action, and a planet can have more than one defender. Choose a planet first, then stage one or more eligible forces there. On confirmation, each actual landing must still be selected from a **fresh engine offer**.

## Starting point and constraints

- `SpaceCombatOverlay.tsx`, `2026-09-27-SPACE_COMBAT_OVERLAY_STATES_PLAN.md`, `2026-09-26-SPACE_COMBAT_CARDS_PLAN.md`, and `web/e2e/space_combat_four_views.spec.ts` establish public phase/round identity, live dice and fleet deltas, actor-only reaction controls, odds-request validity, and four independent viewers. `GameShell.tsx` still retains the last space-combat board as a **blocking** completion view; its hand-off must be fixed before testing a continuous invasion.
- `InvasionLandingTray.tsx` already offers sequential `commit_ground_forces` choices. `GameShell` otherwise routes ground-related decisions to generic UI. `BoardView` has `combat` for space but no invasion view. `DecisionContext.space_battle` is deliberately space-only.
- `InvasionWindow` (`crates/ti4-engine/src/invasion.rs`) rolls bombardment when it opens; targets may require a choice on coexisting planets. Custodians/payment precedes commitment; each landing can open a Parley reaction. Space cannon defense resolves after commitment. The active ground window's `fight_ground_combat_round` choice runs pre-roll Dunlain Reaper deploy/payment, rolls **both** sides, and applies hits automatically in one resolution, with Fire Team/reroll reactions along the way. Its ground casualties are normally not offered as choices: `assign_ground_casualty` exists in the separate synchronous ground-combat path. The overlay must not invent sustain/casualty buttons or expect a space-combat-style hit-assignment pause.
- `crates/ti4-training/src/battle_arena.rs` already has `GroundSide` and `ground_fight` (bombardment, defense, simultaneous rounds, Harrow, automatic sustain/cheap-first casualties). `ti4-advisor` currently exposes only space `/battle`; `buildBattleRequest` sets `in_progress: true` for **space** combat. Ground odds need a dedicated request/response or mode, using the ground simulator with correct step boundaries. A percentage from the space endpoint would be wrong.
- `2026-09-26-GAME_ACTION_BATCHES_PLAN.md` supplies a private-replay, single-actor atomic batch path for specified baskets. Start with a **pipelined** landing draft: confirm the chosen forces, then consume one fresh offer at a time (not one speculative choice list reused for the whole draft). Do not auto-answer a new actor's Parley or another reaction; pause the pipeline and let the actor resolve it, then revalidate the remaining draft. An atomic landing batch may be added later using that existing executor if latency warrants it; such a batch cannot span another human's required decision. `done_committing` stops **further landings**; it does not cancel the invasion or halt already-committed forces' defense/ground combats. The engine offers `fight_ground_combat_round` before each round but no general stop/pause-invasion action or optional hit-resolution steps. Keep automatic stages automatic.

## Contract and implementation slices

### 1. Establish an authoritative invasion boundary and public view

1. Add an engine-owned active-invasion marker when entering `Aftermath::Invading` in `game.rs` (early enough for `INVASION_BEGAN` cards), clear it on hand-off to production or action completion. Include system, invader, activation/battle sequence, and the current planet/ground-opponent occurrence where relevant. Do not infer an invasion from units on a planet, a space battle, or a generic planet-targeted reaction. Propagate association to nested reaction/card-selection/Sabotage/payment/target choices without replacing their original planet target; keep it separate from `space_battle`. Handle undo/replay and consecutive invasions in the same system.
2. Project `board.invasion` independently of pending choice subtype in `projection.rs`, `protocol/view.rs`, and `web/src/protocol/types.ts`. Start with `system_id`, `invasion_seq`, `invader`, presentation phase, ordered relevant planets, current planet, defender(s), ground-battle sequence and local round, plus public result/control. The planet identity belongs on **every** roll/hit/snapshot record; never combine dice across planets or attach them to the game's action-phase round.
3. Publish the minimum durable public evidence for visible pauses: landing totals and on-planet forces (including damage and structures), per-planet bombardment/space-cannon/ground dice where available, hits produced, and the before/after ground-force snapshot for the _current_ bombardment or round. Ground rolls staged by `roll_ground` are grouped by combat threshold, not reliably by unit type: either project accurate threshold groups or record exact unit dice at the engine roll site; do not claim that a specific infantry rolled a specific face without evidence. Record the post-reroll values, and distinguish bombardment, space cannon defense, ordinary ground rolls, and Harrow. When an automatic step crosses several stages before the next network snapshot, publish a bounded, phase/planet-scoped **last resolved step** summary or public event; do not manufacture an acknowledgement choice just to expose a transient phase. Clear old dice and annotations on each new planet/round/undo.
4. Keep option IDs, hands, eligibility, outstanding private constraints, and the invader's **unsubmitted landing draft** actor-only. Public phase, already-committed forces, dice, unit losses, control changes, and actual played-card facts may be shown to all. Reuse the existing shared server decision-fact/event path for readable landing, choice-of-bombardment-target, custodians, ground-round, and planet-control history.

### 3. Add projected landing odds with a strict validity window

1. Expose a dedicated ground-odds advisor endpoint or typed mode backed by existing `GroundSide`/`ground_fight`, with bounded simulation count and server-side unit validation. Reuse the response shape and fetch/card/abort/key logic where sensible, not the space-fleet request or its fleet-power heuristic. During commitment, simulate the **real selected planet** with the invader's ground forces already there **plus the local unsubmitted draft**, versus its current ground-force defender; re-run as the draft grows or shrinks. Bombardment has already resolved, so exclude it. Space cannon defense has **not** fired: include applicable standing defenders' guns against the projected landed troops (or explicitly label/unavailable if relevant gun modifiers cannot be modeled), and include modeled Harrow only when legal. Label the result `Projected odds if these forces land` and make clear it excludes cards, future reactions such as Parley, optional Dunlain Reaper deploy and unmodeled modifiers. It is a conditional preview, **not a guarantee** the draft will land or an estimate of system-wide control.
2. Show the preview while choosing commitments, before `done_committing`, only for a selected planet with a positive projected invading ground force and a definite opposing ground-force defender. With no opposing ground forces, show `No ground battle expected` instead of a fabricated percentage. If more than one rival ground force owner stands there, use the engine's controller-first defender for the immediate battle and label which opponent is modeled; do not imply it predicts optional later fights. As the pipeline commits units, shift them from draft to public on-planet counts without double-counting; recompute on a board change, Parley, or a changed defender. After commitment, optionally retain a **separate** pre-round ground-only odds estimate if the same projected input is still valid, but hide all estimates during known dice/hit resolution, after a win, or when the battle/planet changes. The `fight_ground_combat_round` offer is before optional Dunlain Reaper deploy/payment; never imply that unmodeled future deploy is included.
3. Key each request by invasion identity, planet, opponent, phase, public committed forces and damage, **draft** unit counts/damage, faction, standing guns and modeled modifiers, plus timeline generation; abort or ignore late results across draft edits, reactions, landing, next planet, undo/replay, and completion. Display fresh percentages only for the matching draft/board; pending/failed requests show `Calculating…` / `Odds unavailable`, never the old value or the space overlay's fleet-power fallback. A local draft must not be sent in another viewer's game snapshot; only the invader sees its preview until forces actually land.

## Invasion-only edge cases and resolved decisions

1. **Landing UX:** choose a planet first, stage several forces, then pipeline one fresh offered landing per request. Pauses for Parley or custodians payments are normal. A partially executed pipeline is visible and resumable after revalidation; an atomic landing batch is deferred.
2. **Odds:** projected ground-combat/capture odds during commitment for `already landed + draft` on the chosen planet, incorporating pre-ground-combat space cannon defense where modeled; no pre-bombardment or unrelated planet/system prediction. Update as forces are added, removed, actually committed, or returned by reactions.
3. **Ground hit assignment:** show the engine's automatic sustains/casualties. Do not add player assignment choices while the live invasion engine does not offer them.
4. **Multiple planets/coexisting owners:** show a series of planet-local battles, with a controller-first defender and an optional fight against remaining coexisters. Cover these in a separate focused scenario; keep the primary four-view invasion slim.
5. **No battle / special transitions:** bombardment can eliminate all defenders before landing; planetary shield, Conventions of War, Disable, DMZ, or a structure-only planet can suppress combat. Custodians can gate landing and award a point; space cannon defense can wipe committed troops; mutual ground annihilation leaves the former controller in place. Each outcome needs a readable planet result rather than a fabricated win percentage.
6. **Visibility of very fast steps:** show a short public result summary for automatic bombardment, space-cannon fire, and ground rounds. `done_committing` ends landing but is not a stop-invasion control; do not add artificial pause/acknowledgement choices. Use the existing round-start choice when there is a next round.
